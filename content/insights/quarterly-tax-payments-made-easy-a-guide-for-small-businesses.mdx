Managing tax payments is one of the biggest challenges for small businesses.

Under Cameroon’s **comprehensive tax regime**, eligible businesses must pay taxes **quarterly**.

This simplifies the process and reduces administrative burdens. But how does it work? Let’s break it down.

### **Quarterly Tax Payments**

Businesses under the comprehensive tax system do not need to file **monthly tax declarations**.

Instead, they are required to make **four tax payments per year**, based on their previous year’s turnover.

### **Quarterly Payment Schedule**

Each payment is due **15 days after the end of each quarter**:

- **Q1:** January - March (**Payment due by April 15**)

- **Q2:** April - June (**Payment due by July 15**)

- **Q3:** July - September (**Payment due by October 15**)

- **Q4:** October - December (**Payment due by January 15** of the following year)

### **How to Calculate Your Quarterly Tax**

Your business's annual tax is determined based on its **previous year’s turnover** and is divided into four equal payments.

Example: If your **annual tax** is **200,000 FCFA**, your quarterly payments will be **50,000 FCFA** each.

For details on tax rates, refer to [**Comprehensive Tax Rates Explained**](https://openhubdigital.com/comprehensive-tax-rates-explained/).

### **How to Pay Your Quarterly Tax**

The government requires businesses to make tax payments **electronically** through designated payment systems. Steps include:

1. **Log into the online tax payment portal.**

3. **Declare your tax amount** based on your assigned turnover bracket.

5. **Make the payment online** using a registered mobile money or bank transfer.

7. **Receive an electronic receipt** as proof of payment.

For detailed steps on electronic tax filing, check **How to Declare and Pay Comprehensive Tax Electronically**.

### **Penalties for Late Payments**

Failure to pay by the due date results in **penalties**, including:

- A **10% fine** for late payment.

- Interest charges for extended delays.

- Risk of **business closure** if non-compliance persists.

Learn more about avoiding penalties in **How to Avoid Tax Penalties and Stay Compliant**.

### **Benefits of Quarterly Tax Payments**

✅ **Less Administrative Burden** – No need for monthly tax declarations.

  
✅ **Predictable Payments** – Businesses can plan their finances efficiently.

  
✅ **Encourages Compliance** – Easier tax obligations reduce the risk of non-compliance.

  
✅ **Improves Cash Flow Management** – Payments are spread across the year.

For small businesses under the **comprehensive tax regime**, quarterly tax payments offer a simple, predictable way to manage tax obligations.

Business that pay their taxes on time can stay compliant while focusing on growth.
