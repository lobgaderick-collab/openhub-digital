### What Constitutes Taxable Income from Financial Assets, Deposits, and Securities?

Section 41 specifies several sources of income that are considered taxable under personal income tax. These include:

1. **Financial Assets:** Includes mortgage debts, preferential debts, or unsecured debts that are not classified under bonds or government stocks.

3. **Sight or Fixed Term Deposits:** Interest and other proceeds from deposits held with financial institutions.

5. **Cash Guarantees:** Returns or interest earned from cash guarantees provided for various purposes.

7. **Current Accounts:** Income from current accounts maintained with financial institutions.

#### Examples of Taxable Income

- **Interest from Fixed Deposits:** If you have a fixed deposit with a bank and earn interest, this interest is taxable.

- **Proceeds from Mortgage Debts:** Any back payments or interest received from mortgage debts are considered taxable income.

- **Income from Current Accounts:** Interest or any other proceeds from current accounts are subject to personal income tax.

### Why Is This Income Taxable?

The tax code categorizes these incomes as taxable to ensure comprehensive tax coverage of various income sources. This is part of the effort to fairly assess the income of all taxpayers based on the diverse financial instruments they might use.

### How to Calculate Taxable Income

To calculate your taxable income from these sources:

1. **Sum Up All Income:** Add all interest, back payments, and proceeds from your financial assets, deposits, and securities.

3. **Report Accurately:** Ensure you report this income accurately on your tax declaration to avoid penalties.

5. **Seek Professional Guidance:** Consult a tax professional for precise calculation and compliance.

#### Example Calculation

Suppose you earned CFAF 200,000 in interest from a fixed deposit, CFAF 100,000 from mortgage debt, and CFAF 50,000 from a current account in a year. Your total taxable income from these sources would be CFAF 350,000. This amount should be included in your personal income tax declaration.

### Key Points to Remember

- Income from various financial assets, including deposits and current accounts, is taxable.

- Ensure accurate reporting to comply with tax regulations.

- Consider consulting a professional for tailored advice.
