Section 4 of the General Tax Code outlines specific exemptions from Company Tax. These exemptions are for entities and activities that contribute to Cameroon’s economic, social, and educational development. These exemptions aim to support key sectors while encouraging compliance.

### **Entities and Activities Exempt from Company Tax**

1. **Co-operative Societies in Agriculture and Livestock**:
    - Includes co-operatives involved in the production, processing, and sale of agricultural and livestock products.
    
    - **Exclusions**:
        - Retail sales distinct from the co-operative’s main premises.
        
        - Processing unrelated to agricultural purposes or for products not meant for human or animal consumption.

3. **Agricultural Unions and Mutual Credit Funds**:
    - Organizations that offer resources for agricultural development, including mutual credit funds supporting farmers.

5. **Non-Profit Educational Institutions**:
    - Private educational establishments operating without profit motives are exempt from Company Tax.

7. **Investment Funds**:
    - Open-end investment companies (SICAV) and mutual credit funds operating within their legal activities.

9. **Economic Interest Groups (EIGs)**:
    - Profits distributed to natural persons as members of EIGs are exempt from Company Tax.

### **Conditions for Exemption**

To benefit from these exemptions, entities must:

1. **Operate Within Legal Guidelines**:
    - Activities must align with the provisions governing the organization or sector.

3. **Maintain Separate Accounting**:
    - Entities conducting both taxable and exempt activities must keep separate accounts for each.

### **Benefits of Exemptions**

1. **Support for Vital Sectors**:
    - Encourages growth in agriculture, education, and non-profit activities.

3. **Reduced Tax Burden**:
    - Eligible organizations can reinvest savings into their core operations.

5. **Encouragement for Compliance**:
    - Simplifies compliance for non-profit and mission-driven entities.

### **How to Claim Exemptions**

1. **Register with Tax Authorities**:
    - Ensure your organization is legally registered and recognized as eligible for exemptions.
