Discover MTN WANDA, a range of personalized bundles designed to give you a Mega bonus for everything you need. With MTN WANDA, enjoy exceptional value on:

- **Local Calls:** Make calls to all networks.
- **International Calls:** Stay connected with friends and family abroad.
- **SMS:** Send messages without worrying about costs.
- **Internet:** Browse the web with added data benefits.

### Eligibility:

All prepaid customers on MTN Go tariff plans are eligible for MTN WANDA bundles.

### Subscription:

Getting started with MTN WANDA is simple. Dial [\*123\*11#](tel:*123*11#) and select your preferred bundle based on your desired validity.

### Balance Check:

To check your balance, dial [\*123\*11\*99#](tel:*123*11*99#).
