Marketing has changed so much in recent years with many startups adopting digital marketing in replacement for traditional marketing which they find expensive. This made me to come up with the best digital marketing tools that I have tested with great success.

Not all digital marketing tools are that effective. Implementing a digital marketing strategy may turn into a big disappointment. You need to apply a strategy that is effective and can generate positive results quickly.

You may have been wondering on what digital marketing strategy you wish to put in place to boost the growth of your startup. After reading this 5 digital marketing tools, you will get a clear picture of the ones you can apply for your startup.

1. **Social Media Marketing:** Nowadays, most young entrepreneurs go in for social media as the main marketing tool for their startup. For those who have not yet used this tool, they should start thinking of it. Social media has played a very important role in marketing startups. For Africa, social media marketing is the most important marketing strategy that startups use.
    
    Social media marketing increases startup exposure and brings in traffic, develops customer loyalty, generate leads, improves search engine ranking as well as establishes a leadership thought.
    
    Not all businesses that adopt social media marketing are doing it the right way. Some of them make **big mistakes** that doesn’t not help in the growth of their businesses. Such mistakes can be avoided by choosing the right **social media channel** before developing a **social media strategy**.[![/images/copy-of-small-business-flyer-template.jpg

3. **Content Marketing:** Content marketing is one of the most cost-effective strategies startups can use. They say _“Content is King”_. A good content marketing strategy will in the long run turn your brand into an authority which will help widen your reach significantly as well as increase your search engine rankings.
    
4. **Search Engine Optimization:** One of my clients visited me and complained that he has checked his ranking on Google and his website is no where to be found. I checked out the website and it was awesome with everything in place. That doesn’t mean visitors will come.

5. **Pay-Per-Click (PPC):** PPC is a marketing method where by marketers use search engine advertising to bring in visitors and potential customers to their website. Pay-per-click is very powerful in generating sales-ready leads.
    
    Startups could go in for PPC at the early stage when their websites are not yet ranked high on search engine. Pay-per-click can help you reach your customers at the right time, has a high return on investment. This happens only when those that are interested click on the ads. The most important advantage is that you have control of how much you want to spend and you wont have to wait for the results.
