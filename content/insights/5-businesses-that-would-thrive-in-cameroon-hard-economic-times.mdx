It's good to think about business ideas or ventures that will still be fruitful even in times of recession. In Cameroon, some businesses will definitely thrive even when things get tough. Let's now look into these five resilient business opportunities.

##### **Home Security Services**

People are most likely to be worried about their security, especially with deepening economic challenges. Many families and lots of businesses are contemplating investment in security services due to the increasing levels of crime.

Offering home security systems, surveillance cameras, or security personnel is an idea for a strategic business. Safety is always paramount, particularly when uncertainty looms.

##### **Daycare and Education Services**

It is shown that an increasing percentage of households have both parents working to cater to their livelihoods. Considering this, there is an ever-increasing need for assured daycare services, under which parents can safely leave their kids while off to work.

Notably, in the aspect of tuition and other educational support services, parents find it essential to invest in these areas to guarantee their kids are fully covered academically. A daycare center or tutoring business if you're really passionate about education.

##### **Healthcare and Wellness**

Health will always be in demand, no matter the economic condition. A clinic, pharmacy, or wellness center just be one of the most stable options in business. The trend now is for people to consider both their physical and mental well-being; hence, fitness classes and mental health support are becoming quite popular. During tough times, managing one's health becomes a priority.

##### **Agriculture**

The economy of Cameroon has always been pegged in agriculture. Small farming in vegetables or poultry can assure steady income even in the toughest periods. Local food production will save you from possible risks of failing global supply chains, with the products vital for the most important needs. People will always need to eat; hence, agriculture is a very steady industry.

##### **Essential Goods and Services**

People will cut down on luxuries, but the basic necessities like food, hygiene, and clothing never stop being in use. Starting a business for such needs, like a small grocery store or opening an online marketplace or even a delivery service of essentials, will guarantee that you always have demand for what you offer.
