## Access MTN Internet Bundles and Enjoy 10 Days of Data Bonus

Take advantage of MTN internet bundles and enjoy fantastic data bonuses. Choose the plan that suits your needs and stay connected with ease. Here’s how to access and subscribe:

- **Dial the Code:** Use [\*157#](tel:*157#) to access internet bundles.
- **myMTN Mobile Application:** Use the app to purchase your bundles.
- **MTN MoMo:** Buy bundles via MTN MoMo by dialing [\*126#](tel:*126#).

Here are some data bundle options available:

- **1GB for 500F:** Valid for 1 day. Subscribe by dialing [\*157\*1\*5#](tel:*157*1*5#).
- **3GB for 1,000F:** Valid for 1 day. Subscribe by dialing [\*157\*1\*6#](tel:*157*1*6#).
- **6GB for 3,000F:** Valid for 7 days. Subscribe by dialing [\*157\*2\*5#](tel:*157*2*5#).
