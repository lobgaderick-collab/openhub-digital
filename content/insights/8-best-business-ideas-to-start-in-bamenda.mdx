Bamenda is the regional capital of the North West Region of Cameroon. It’s also the 5th largest city in the country after Douala, Yaounde, Garoua, and Kousseri with a population of 393,835 as of 2021 (See [Geonames](https://www.geonames.org/CM/largest-cities-in-cameroon.html)).

The entire North West Region has a population of 1,900,547 (2013) and also ranked as the 5th largest region in Cameroon.

The economy of the city is mostly into processing of agricultural produce, food processing, education, handicraft, tourism, transport and construction.

Given the crisis in the North West and South West Regions, business activities in the city have declined.

Many outdoor businesses have shutdown, and those left are not working full-time due to the frequent _ghost towns_ and insecurity.

Many have lost their businesses, jobs and other means of earning income due to the crisis.

However, that does not mean they can’t explore other ‘safe’ ways to earn extra income without exposing themselves to high risks.

Given the unrest in the town and the entire region, most of the business ideas I will share are those that can easily be carried out in the safety of your home. I am talking about businesses that come with little movements and involves little start-up capital.

### 1\. Interior Designing

**Home Based:** Can be operated from home

**Part-Time:** Can be operated part-time

**Online** **Marketing****?** Can be marketed online

What’s interior designing all about? Interior design is about making homes or workplaces look more beautiful. This is done by selecting and blending colors, lighting, furniture and fabrics.

Interior design is a business that grows alongside growth in the construction industry.

Bamenda is a city that has seen growth in construction in the past years, even though the crisis has brought in a decline.

However, even in the midst of the crisis, you can still carry out activities by advertising your business online, targeting other cities like Bafoussam, Douala and Yaounde.

The business is especially suited for people who want to run their business from home. The most important thing you need for this business is to have the skills to execute it and how to deal with clients.

Interior designing is a thriving business that can be done in every city or town in Cameroon. Many workplaces and homes like to hire experienced home decorators.

To make it big in this sector, you need to study all aspects of the business and come up with some clever marketing strategies.

Your clients include both residential and commercial property owners. These include:

\- Hotels

\- Restaurants

\- Offices

\- Residences

### 5\. Niche-based e-commerce Store

**Home Based:** Can be operated from home or in a Coworking Space

**Part-Time:** Can be operated part-time

**Online** **Marketing****?** Can be marketed online

If your dream is to sit in the comfort of your home and run a profitable business online, then you can think of starting a viable and legit e-commerce business in Bamenda.

Your business is legit when you sell products that are legally accepted to be bought and sold within Cameroon. Also, the business should be a [legally registered business](https://openhubdigital.com/what-is-a-registered-business/).

There are a lot of [disadvantages that come with you running an unregistered business](https://openhubdigital.com/caught-running-an-unregistered-business/). If you already have a business that is not yet registered, think twice. Don’t fall into the trap of those who run non-registered businesses and also don’t miss the advantages of running one.

Just like we’re unique and different from each other, so our interests, skills and strengths. If you’re looking for a business to start, it’s important that you go for what works best for you.

An e-commerce business refers to the buying and selling of goods or services using the Internet. This also involves the transfer of data and money to carry out transactions.

Also called electronic or Internet commerce, it’s the sale of physical products online as well as any commercial transaction that’s facilitated via the Internet.

With the ever increasing presence on the Internet in Cameroon, the number of people who buy things online is also increasing.

In this case, starting an e-commerce store means you have a lot of potential buyers waiting for your products now, and more in the future.

This makes it a worthwhile investment for anyone with little capital and wishing to find a good source of income.

With an e-commerce store, you can make money by selling products online with [dropshipping](https://openhubdigital.com/launch-a-dropshipping-business-in-cameroon-with-very-little-startup-capital/) and affiliate marketing.

There are millions of products out there you can sell from companies that offer affiliate marketing opportunities.

You can also sell products from local shops around you online. This means you don’t need to keep stock of your own, but negotiate with these local shops.

All what you need to do is to [build an Online store](https://openhubdigital.com/the-best-web-design-services-in-cameroon/). **Part-Time:** Can be operated part-time

**Online** **Marketing****?** Can be marketed online

Cutting and styling your hair is one of the things that we do at least once a month. However, due to the crisis in the North West and South West regions of Cameroon, businesses have not been going on normally in Bamenda.
