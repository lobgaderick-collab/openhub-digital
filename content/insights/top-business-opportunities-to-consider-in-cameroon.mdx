Cameroon is a country located in central Africa, with a population of over 26 million people. Cameroon has a diverse economy, with key sectors including agriculture, mining, energy, telecommunications, transportation, and tourism. The country has a growing entrepreneurial culture, and there are many business opportunities available for those who are interested in starting a business in Cameroon.

### [Agriculture](https://openhubdigital.com/investment-opportunities-in-sorghum-maize-and-cassava-production/)

Cameroon has fertile agricultural land and produces a variety of crops, including coffee, cocoa, bananas, rubber, and palm oil. The agricultural sector accounts for approximately 17.25% of Cameroon's GDP as of 2021 and employs around 60% of the country's population \[Source: [Statista](http://statista.com): [Distribution of gross domestic product (GDP) across economic sectors from 2011 to 2021](https://www.statista.com/statistics/446567/cameroon-gdp-distribution-across-economic-sectors/)\]. Cameroon's agricultural sector has significant potential for growth, with opportunities for small and large-scale farming, as well as processing and exporting of agricultural products.

One potential business opportunity in the agricultural sector is farming. You could start a business in growing and selling crops such as cocoa, coffee, bananas, and rubber. Cameroon is the fifth-largest producer of cocoa in the world, and cocoa farming is a significant contributor to the country's economy.

Another potential business opportunity in the agricultural sector is processing and exporting of agricultural products. ### Transportation

Cameroon has a growing transportation sector, with opportunities in trucking, logistics, and passenger transportation. The transportation sector accounts for approximately 4% of Cameroon's GDP and is expected to continue to grow in the coming years \[Source: [African Development Bank Group](https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/document/cameroon-transport-sector-support-programme-phase-2-rehabilitation-of-the-yaounde-bafoussam-babadjou-road-development-of-the-grand-zambi-kribi-road-and-development-of-the-maroua-bogo-pouss-road-appraisal-report-93374)\].

One potential business opportunity in the transportation sector is providing transport services. You could start a business providing transportation services for goods or people. There is a significant demand for transportation services in Cameroon, particularly in the rural areas where there is limited public transportation.

Another potential business opportunity in the transportation sector is logistics. You could start a business providing logistics solutions for companies that need to transport goods. Cameroon has several ports and airports that serve as transport hubs for the region, making it an ideal location for a logistics business.

### Energy

Cameroon has a significant potential for renewable energy, such as solar, wind, and hydro power. The energy sector accounts for approximately 3.7% of Cameroon's GDP as of 2021 and is expected to continue to grow in the coming years \[Source: IRENA: [Cameroon Energy Profile](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Statistics/Statistical_Profiles/Africa/Cameroon_Africa_RE_SP.pdf)\].

One potential business opportunity in the energy sector is providing energy solutions. You could start a business providing solar panels or wind turbines. Cameroon has a significant potential for solar power, with an average of 5.5 hours of sunlight per day. You could also start a business in providing hydro power solutions, as Cameroon has several rivers that could be used for hydro power.

Another potential business opportunity in the energy sector is providing energy efficiency solutions. You could start a business providing energy audits and recommendations for companies and individuals looking to reduce their energy consumption. Cameroon has a significant potential for energy efficiency, as many buildings and facilities are not designed with energy efficiency in mind.

### [Tourism](https://openhubdigital.com/business-idea-tour-agency-for-local-tourism/)

Cameroon has many natural attractions, such as national parks, wildlife reserves, and beaches. Tourism is a growing industry in Cameroon, with business opportunities for hotels, tour companies, and restaurants.

One potential business opportunity in the tourism industry is running a hotel. You could start a business in running a hotel, catering to tourists visiting Cameroon's national parks, wildlife reserves, and beaches. Cameroon has many natural attractions that attract tourists, and there is a growing demand for quality accommodation.

Another potential business opportunity in the tourism industry is running a tour company. You could start a business providing tours of Cameroon's national parks, wildlife reserves, and beaches. There is a significant demand for tour companies in Cameroon, particularly for visitors who are looking for a guided experience of the country's natural attractions.

Conclusion

Cameroon has many business opportunities available for those who are interested in starting a business in the country. The key sectors include agriculture, transportation, energy, telecommunications, and tourism. 

There are business opportunities for small and large-scale businesses, as well as for those who are interested in providing services or products. It's important to do your own research and due diligence to determine which business ideas or opportunity is the best fit for your skills, experience, and interests, and to comply with all local regulations and laws.
