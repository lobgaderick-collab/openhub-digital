3ConeX Limited is a progressive company that offers training on 21st-century skills that is committed to bringing the value of digital skills, technology and STEM to a generation that no longer needs excuses or limitations.

Its flagship courses in coding, robotics and data science are set to deliver world-class training and services to kids aged 7 to 17.

Presently, they are providing training in the fields of coding, robotics, and data science to kids. We are presently located in Bonamoussadi, Douala, with the aim of opening other training centres in major metropolitan and rural areas in Cameroon.

They provide courses in programming, artificial intelligence, software development, cloud computing, machine learning, robotics and data science. Students can choose from beginner, intermediate or advanced level courses, depending on their experience and skills.

3ConeX Limited has helped many kids obtain coding skills since it started activities six months ago with a coding boot camp, HolidayCode for Kids.

With experienced and qualified instructors, the kids are confident that they are receiving the best possible training in the various fields mentioned above.

### The vision of 3ConeX Limited

Their Vision is to equip the young generation with a bright tech future, and build up young entrepreneurs who will become Artificial Intelligence experts (AI), Software developers, Cloud developers, Machine learning experts(ML), Robotics, Data Scientists, etc.

To one of their co-founders, Emmanuel Okonkwo, _“__this_ _shall be achieved by providing students with high-tech training from the very basic level to advanced levels through partnerships with coaches and trainers from all over the world.”_

### Key Activities / Services of 3ConeX

#### Training Kids in Coding & Robotics

3ConeX Limited provides various levels of courses in coding and robotics. These courses are designed to deliver the best training to kids aged 7 to 10.

They run a 9-month coding program that is aimed at giving the kids more insight into coding, building on their, creativity skills, problem-solving, design thinking skills and more.

They plan to also delve into the advanced coding world using different programming languages such as python, Coffee Script, or Java, learn about robotics and more. This will enable the trainees to start learning how to develop their own applications, games and software at an early stage.

At the end of our courses, students/Trainees will have developed solid creativity skills, strategic thinking, analytical thinking, and logical thinking skills and be exposed to real-life challenges and the ways of solving these challenges to achieve success.

Training takes place every Saturday, on-site for those who live around Bonamoussadi, Douala and on Zoom for the rest of the world.

Laptops and free internet connection are provided to those who live around our centres. For now, we have only one centre in Douala.

They plan to open more centres in different parts of the country to enable children who have access to computers and the internet to take part in our course.

Their courses are designed to teach children the basics of programming, robotics, artificial intelligence, software development, cloud computing, machine learning, robotics and data science.

As a parent, you may be concerned with the career choices your child will make when they grow up. Hence, at 3ConeX Limited, aims to give children a better starting point in life.

For more information on 3ConeX Limited, please visit their website, [3conex.com](http://3conex.com/).

#### Coworking Space

3ConeX also runs a co-working space and business centre at their office in Bonamoussadi. Their office space has a fully-equipped office and shared offices with an open-plan, collaborative environment for entrepreneurs to study, learn, share ideas, work, and create.

Training Room: Their training room is equipped with a projector, a whiteboard, and computers. Here, users can access our training material, watch tutorials, collaborate with their peers, and enjoy the facilities.

Laptops, generator, free coffee and free internet connection is provided to all users of our shared office space.
