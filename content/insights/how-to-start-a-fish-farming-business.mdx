#### Closed culture fish farming

It is a practice in which fish are raised in tanks and fed with feed that is crafted with additives to encourage their growth. The advantages of closed culture fish farming is that it is easy to manage and control the environment, and it is a more efficient use of space than open culture (where the fish are grown in large ponds).

This method is adopted by many fast food and restaurant chains in order to cut down on food waste.

#### Open culture fish farming

Open Culture fish farming involves growing fish in open, non-sealed environments. It is a type of fish farming where the fish are not kept in a bag or cage. Instead, the fish are kept in an open environment, and are able to swim around and interact with each other.

The fish are fed on a diet of their own waste and organic products, and they are given a regular supply of fresh water. The result is that they grow faster, live longer, and are much more attractive to the consumer.

This is a great way to avoid problems associated with keeping fish in large tanks, such as disease and stress-related behavior, and it can also help to keep the fish more healthy.

Closed culture system has low maintenance cost. Open culture system needs less care.

### Step 3. Learn the skills required for Fish Farming

You need to learn some basic skills and techniques in order to start a fish farm. You need to learn some of the fish farming skills. Below are some of the skills you need to learn.

**1\. Fertilizer Management.**

Fish fertilizer management is essential to the success of fish farming. This is a lot of work, so it is very important to have a management system in place. You can prevent the loss of nutrients and fish by using the right fertilizer management.

**2\. Fish Handling.**

Fish Handling is a term used to describe the activities performed by fish farmers to grow fish. The most common fish handling activities involve starting and maintaining the cultures, feeding the fish, and harvesting the fish.

**3\. Fish Tanks Maintenance.**

Fish tanks are a great way to keep your fish happy at home, and many people enjoy keeping fish as a hobby. However, fish tanks can be a bit of a challenge to maintain.

However, fish tanks come with many maintenance requirements. Maintenance is the act of keeping a fish tank clean and healthy and includes:

- Checking for water quality - to avoid problems, you should be checking the water quality every day.

- Water changes - regular water changes are needed to ensure that fish have fresh, oxygenated water to live in.

- Cleaning - you need to keep your tank clean, especially at the start. You will need to remove the filter regularly to clean it.

- Lighting - a tank needs to be well lit.

**4\. Fish Feeding.**

In order to raise fish, it is important to feed them the right amount of food. Too much food will make them obese and lazy, and not enough will result in hunger and starvation.

**5\. Fish Health Management.**

Fish health management is one of the most important aspects of fish culture. In order to ensure that fish remain healthy and productive, it is important to monitor and maintain the health of the pond or tank. You also need to monitor the water quality frequently.

**6\. Fish Diseases Management.**

Fish diseases are a big problem for the aquaculture industry. It can be both dangerous and costly for fish farmers. Bacteria, viruses and protozoa cause these diseases. They can cause massive losses, and put a damper on the industry as a whole.

It is therefore important for fish farmers to put aside preventive measures to fight any outbreak. The best way to do this is to have a good fish disease management plan that takes into account the various factors that can cause fish disease.

**7\. Fish Purging.**

Fish purging is a method of removing unwanted fish from a tank. It is a complex process that requires careful planning and execution, so only undertake it if you have a lot of experience with fish.

To do a fish purging, you will need a siphon, a suction pump, a water pump and some containers. If you are doing the purging while the fish are in a live water environment, you will also need a small pump, some containers and a net.

**8\. Fish Processing.**

Preparing the fish for the market is a very important part of the process. Fish preparation for the market involves many steps. It is essential that you understand how to prepare the fish. This process should not take many days. This is because the more days that you process your fish, the better it will be for you.

The farmer can freeze, dry or sell the fish fresh. Frozen is the best because it is more affordable and more convenient for you. The packing of fish is vital in that it preserves the freshness and quality of the fish. Buyers usually go a reputable dealer who has a good packing facility.
