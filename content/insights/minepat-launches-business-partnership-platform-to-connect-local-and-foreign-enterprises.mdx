The **Ministry of the Economy, Planning, and Regional Development (MINEPAT)** has introduced a **Business Partnership Platform** aimed at helping local businesses establish international collaborations. This initiative comes as part of the government’s efforts to integrate Cameroonian enterprises into the global market and strengthen economic ties with foreign investors.

According to MINEPAT, the platform is designed to:

- **Identify the needs of foreign companies** seeking local partners in Cameroon.

- **Provide local businesses** with structured opportunities to connect with international firms.

- **Facilitate negotiations and partnerships** that align with Cameroon’s economic development goals.

As part of this initiative, MINEPAT is **conducting a data collection exercise from February 3 to 28, 2025**, to gather information on local businesses interested in forming partnerships. The collected data will help define the profiles of foreign companies best suited for collaboration with Cameroonian enterprises.

### **How Businesses Can Participate**

Local businesses are invited to fill out the **Business Partnership Data Collection Form** on the official MINEPAT website. The form requires companies to provide details on their activities, areas of interest for partnerships, and specific collaboration needs.

The form can be accessed at [**MINEPAT Business Partnership Form**](https://minepat-cm.org/formulaire.html).

### **Government’s Commitment to Economic Growth**

The Cameroonian government, through MINEPAT, emphasizes that this platform is a key step towards **enhancing trade, boosting investments, and fostering sustainable partnerships** between local and foreign enterprises. By reducing barriers to international business collaboration, the initiative aims to create new growth opportunities for Cameroonian businesses.

MINEPAT encourages all eligible businesses to take advantage of this opportunity by registering on the platform before the deadline. This initiative is expected to **bridge the gap between Cameroonian enterprises and global markets**, ultimately contributing to the country’s economic development.
