**Vettelicious Cakes & Cuties** is a home-based cake business based in Yaounde owned by [Yvette Mbuh](https://www.facebook.com/yvette.mbuh). Yvette started cake decorating as a hobby, but today, she’s fully into a professional cake decorating business.

**Vettelicious Cakes & Cuties** is a custom cakes and sweet treats business that bakes and decorates cakes for your event, meeting or special occasions to make it memorable and delicious.
