### 2\. Online brokerage

Just like in the real world, brokers are those that make the market. They are those that bring the buyers and sellers together to facilitate transactions. At the end of the day, they will earn a commission either from one or both parties. It could be business-to-consumers (B2C) or business-to-business (B2B). The unique thing about this business model is that they sell products and services through websites. Examples of brokerage models include eBay, Macys.com and we can find [Jumia](http://jumia.com/), [Sellamquick](http://Sellamquick.com/), etc. in Cameroon.

### 3\. Affiliate marketing

This is one of the most popular methods people use to make money online. In this model, you partner with a business to refer visitors or readers to any of its product or service for a commission. In this case, the business pays online retailers a commission for traffic or sales generated from referring the product or service to an external website. Writing content is an added advantage for those who plan to become affiliate marketers. There are many sites out there that offer affiliate marketing opportunities like Amazon Associates and Jumia Affiliate Program in Cameroon.

This type of business model is not very new in Cameroon just that we have been practicing the offline version of it. There are many people out there that earn a living from bonuses they earn usually called _“ndzama”_ by referring clients to other businesses.
