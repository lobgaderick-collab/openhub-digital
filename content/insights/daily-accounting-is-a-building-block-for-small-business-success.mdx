Daily accounting drives growth for small businesses in Cameroon, simplifying tax filing.

Recording daily income and expense for your business involves more than just tallying how much your business made. It's also about understanding how much was spent each day.

As a small business, it should be a discipline. It is a habit that helps in planning, budgeting and preparing your business for growth.

In Cameroon, businesses with a turnover between 10,000,000F and of 50,000,000F are under the simplified tax system. With this system, they need to use [basic cash accounting system](https://openhubdigital.com/simplified-tax-system-monthly-tax-calculator/). With this simplified system, businesses record income or expenses only when money enters or go out respectively.

This method keeps things straightforward. It helps small businesses stay on top of their finances. It does so without over complicating daily processes. Tracking these numbers day by day is tedious, but it’s key to growing and maintaining a healthy business.
