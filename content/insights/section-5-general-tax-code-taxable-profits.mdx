Section 5 of the General Tax Code explains in detail how taxable profits are determined. It applies to entities subject to Company Tax. This section is essential for businesses to understand their obligations and ensure compliance with Cameroon’s tax laws.

### **Definition of Taxable Profits**

Taxable profits refer to the net income derived from all business operations carried out within Cameroon. These include:

1. **Profits from Business Operations**:
    - Revenue generated from regular commercial, industrial, or agricultural activities.

3. **Profits from Asset Transfers**:
    - Income from the transfer of assets during or at the end of the business cycle.

5. **Profits from Related Activities**:
    - Earnings from any gainful activities directly or indirectly linked to the primary operations of the business.

### **Determining Taxable Profits**

1. **Net Profit Calculation**:
    - The net profit is calculated as the difference between the closing and opening net assets of the financial period. This is adjusted for extra contributions and withdrawals made by members during the year.

3. **Valuation of Stocks and Work in Progress**:
    - Stocks are valued at cost price. If the market price is lower than the cost, provisions for depreciation must be made.
    
    - Work in progress is valued at production cost, ensuring precise reflection of the company’s financial position.

5. **Allocation of Expenses**:
    - Only justified and documented expenses are deductible. Invoices must follow the mandatory information specified in the tax code.
    
    - **Reminder**: As outlined in Section 8a, expenses supported by invoices lacking mandatory information are non-deductible. Invoices issued outside the tax authority’s electronic invoicing system are also non-deductible.
