One of the most common question we have to answer our prospective clients is "**How long does it take to register a business in Cameroon?"** According to OHADA Law, the time frame for business registration is short if all required documents are in place.

Under OHADA Law, which governs business formation in Cameroon and other African countries, entrepreneurs can choose from various legal structures like a sole proprietorship, private limited company (SARL), public limited company (SA), or even partnerships.

### Typical Registration Timeframe

On average, to register a business in Cameroon takes from 10-14 working days. Nonetheless, this can vary depending on factors like the type of business structure and the readiness of of your documentation.

For example, registering a sole proprietorship takes less time compared to a private limited company (SARL) because fewer legal steps are involved.

The process begins with gathering necessary documents like the **non-conviction certificate**, identification papers and proof of your business location. Once these are ready, the registration can continue smoothly.

Starting a business in Cameroon comes with important decisions, and one of the first choices you'll need to make is [**deciding on the right legal structure for your business**](https://openhubdigital.com/choose-the-right-business-structure-in-cameroon/).

Choosing the right structure depends on your business goals, investment needs, and personal liability preferences. It’s always best to consult legal and business professionals when making this decision.

### Ways to Speed Up the Process

To guarantee a quick registration, make sure all required documents follow **OHADA Law** and that you submit them to the appropriate authorities like the Chamber of Commerce or a notary public.
