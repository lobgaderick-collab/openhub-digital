**Job Title: Business Intelligence (BI) Specialist with Excel Expertise**

**Location:** Douala, Cameroon

**Salary:** Starting at 250,000 XAF, with potential for increase based on performance

**About Us:**  
The company is a dynamic and forward-thinking organization based in Douala, Cameroon. They are committed to leveraging data-driven insights to fuel their growth and innovation. They are seeking a highly skilled Business Intelligence (BI) Specialist proficient in Excel to join their team and drive their data initiatives.

**Job Description:**  
As a Business Intelligence (BI) Specialist, you will play a crucial role in transforming data into actionable insights. You will be responsible for collecting, analyzing, and visualizing data using advanced Excel techniques. Your work will support key business decisions and contribute to our strategic objectives.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Develop and maintain BI dashboards and reports using Excel

- Analyze complex data sets to identify trends, patterns, and insights

- Create interactive visualizations and pivot tables for data analysis

- Integrate and manage data from various sources using Excel tools

- Collaborate with different departments to understand their data needs and provide solutions

- Ensure data accuracy and integrity in reports and analyses

**Qualifications:**

- Proven experience in Business Intelligence or data analysis

- Proficiency in Excel, including advanced functions, pivot tables, Power Query, and Power Pivot

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

- Ability to work with large data sets and perform complex calculations

- Excellent communication and presentation skills

- Attention to detail and a commitment to data accuracy

**Benefits:**

- Competitive starting salary of 250,000 XAF, with opportunities for salary increases based on performance

- Opportunity to work in a dynamic and innovative environment

- Professional development and growth opportunities

- Collaborative and supportive team culture
