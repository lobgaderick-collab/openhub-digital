Are you looking for ways to create sustainable income streams? Cocoa business ideas may be the answer. Cocoa is a versatile crop that can be used to create a variety of products, from chocolate to cocoa butter.

The government of Cameroon in its 2023 Finance Law has imposed a 10% export duty on cocoa bean exports.

Export duty policies has been a practice used by governments for centuries to protect domestic industries and help small businesses. With this done, it is time for you to take this advantage to invest in the sector.

With the right business plan, you can turn cocoa into a profitable venture.

Cocoa is a crop that is grown in many parts of the world, including Africa, South America, and the Caribbean. Cameroon happens to be the sixth highest cocoa producing country in the world with a total of about 290,000 tons of cocoa beans per year \[Source: [World Atlas](https://www.worldatlas.com/industries/the-top-cocoa-producing-countries-in-the-world.html)\].

It should be noted that the cocoa is a very valuable commodity in Cameroon, and its production is an important source of income for more than 600,000 farmers, especially in the rural communities.

### Cocoa business ideas

When it comes to cocoa business ideas, there are a variety of options. It is a key ingredient in many of our favorite treats, such as chocolate, cocoa butter, and cocoa powder.

You can start a cocoa farm, process cocoa beans, or create products from cocoa. You can also create a cocoa-based business, such as a chocolate shop or a cocoa-based bakery.

### Cocoa farming

There are many different ways to get involved in growing cocoa. Some people buy cocoa from farmers and sell it themselves. Others become involved in the entire process, from planting to harvesting to processing. There are also opportunities to get into cocoa farming as an investor or funding partner.

There is a lot of money to be made from cocoa farming. In 2017, the combined value of cocoa export was $8.6 billion and this is expected to reach $16.32 in 2025 with a compound annual growth rate of 7.3% \[Source: [International Institute for Sustainable Development](https://www.iisd.org/system/files/publications/ssi-global-market-report-cocoa.pdf)\].

Cocoa is a widely consumed product, with production levels of around 4.9 billion tonnes in the 2021/2022 cocoa season.

When it comes to cocoa farming, you will need to consider the climate, soil, and other factors that will affect the quality of the crop. You will also need to consider the cost of labor and equipment. Cocoa farming can be a profitable venture, but it requires a lot of hard work and dedication.

### Cocoa bean processing

Processing cocoa beans is another option for cocoa business ideas. This involves grinding the beans into a powder and then using it to create products such as cocoa butter, cocoa powder, and chocolate. This process requires specialized equipment and knowledge, so it is important to research the process before investing in it.

In 2020, the global cocoa processing market was valued at about $12.4 billion. This amount is expected to increase to $14.6 by 2027.

Cocoa processing business involves the harvest, drying, shelling and blanching of cocoa beans. The beans are then processed to remove the bean hulls, remove the cocoa butter and dry out the beans. After processing, the cocoa beans are sent to various parts of the world to be sold, mostly to European and American companies.

Very few companies process cocoa in Cameroon. Most of the finished products from cocoa are imported, like chocolate, cocoa powder beverages, and cocoa-based beauty products.

With the imposed export duty, exports will reduce, thereby causing a drop in price in the local market. This is situation will help improve local investment in cocoa processing and transformation. It will also attract foreign investment in the area as already existing companies will divert from costly export price and benefit from low prices in the local market.

### Making cocoa-based products

Cocoa-based businesses include companies that are engaged in the production and marketing of cocoa-based products.

Cocoa is a dark, bitter-tasting bean and is a major ingredient in chocolate. It is also the main ingredient in cocoa butter. Cocoa butter is a natural food additive and is used in a variety of products, including lipstick, lip balm, and skin care products.

Cocoa butter is used as a skin conditioner, it helps to improve the moisture content of the skin and it also helps to prevent wrinkles. Cocoa butter is also used in many beauty products, including moisturizing creams. You can also create cocoa-based beverages, such as hot cocoa and cocoa-based ice cream.

### Cocoa packaging & distribution

Cocoa packaging and distribution is a lucrative business idea that can be profitable for entrepreneurs. With clearance from the government, small businesses can purchase finished cocoa products like cocoa butter and cocoa powder for wholesale, from businesses that process cocoa, package them using their own brand before distributing them.

Packaging and distribution of cocoa business ideas require careful planning and execution. It is important to ensure that the cocoa products are properly packaged in order to maintain their freshness and quality.

Additionally, it is important to have an efficient distribution system in place so that the products reach customers quickly and safely. With proper packaging and distribution strategies in place, entrepreneurs can maximize their profits from this business idea.

### Starting a cocoa inputs business

Starting a farm input retail business can be a great way to make some extra money and provide an essential service to cocoa farmers in your community. You can sell various farm inputs like fertilizers, pesticides and other farm equipment which can be sold or leased to farmers. Research different farm inputs that farmers need to find the best prices and quality of products available. Make sure to order enough inventory to meet customer demand and keep your shelves stocked.

You can also register to become an agent for one of the many companies that sell fertilizers, pesticides and other farm equipment for for the production of cocoa.

### Cocoa roasting business

Starting a cocoa bean roasting business is an excellent way to generate revenue while serving a unique purpose in the marketplace. You have the option of either roasting the beans yourself or outsourcing the process, and then you can package and distribute them to local businesses or online customers who are looking for freshly roasted cocoa beans. With the right resources, you can quickly establish yourself as an authority in this niche market.

### Start a cocoa drying business

This involves buying cocoa beans from farmers, drying them, and then selling them to chocolate manufacturers or other buyers. You can also add value to the beans by roasting and grinding them before selling.

You can sell the roasted and ground cocoa beans via local farmers markets or directly to chocolate manufacturers or other buyers.

### Cocoa farm leasing business

Creating a cocoa farm leasing business can be a lucrative venture with the right resources and support. You can lease out farmland to cocoa farmers and provide them with specialized equipment, fertilizers, pesticides, and other necessary supplies to help them grow their crops.

### Consulting business

You can start a consulting business that gives expert advice on cocoa farming techniques and marketing support. This can enable your clients, cocoa farmers, to maximize their cocoa yields.

The cocoa business has the potential to become a sustainable source of income for many entrepreneurs in Cameroon.

With the right strategy, investment, and hard work, your cocoa business idea can be leveraged to create a steady stream of income.

With the right initiative, you can create a business that is financially sustainable and beneficial to their local community.

With the right guidance and commitment, cocoa businesses have the potential to create a lasting, successful business that will benefit not only you, but their community as well.

No matter which cocoa business idea you choose, it is important to create a business plan that outlines your goals and objectives. This will help you stay on track and ensure that your business is successful.
