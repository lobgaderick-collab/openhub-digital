## Navigating Cameroon's Actual Earnings Tax System

In Cameroon, businesses with a robust turnover of XAF50,000,000 and above fall under the jurisdiction of the Actual Earnings Tax System.

This system imposes a taxation mechanism that entails companies paying 2% of their turnover as the principal tax. Additionally, there's an extra council tax, amounting to 10% of the overall tax, making the total tax rate equivalent to 2.2% of the turnover.

### Deciphering the Actual Earnings Tax System

Cameroon's Actual Earnings Tax System applies to businesses with turnovers surpassing XAF50,000,000. Under this system, companies are required to pay a principal tax rate of 2% on their turnover. Additionally, an extra council tax, amounting to 10% of the overall tax, is levied. This combined tax rate totals to 2.2% of the turnover.

### Meeting Monthly Tax Obligations with Precision

For businesses under the Actual Earnings Tax System, the timely declaration and payment of monthly taxes are imperative. According to tax laws, companies must file and remit their taxes by the 15th of each month for the previous month's earnings. Failure to adhere to these deadlines may result in penalties and fines.
