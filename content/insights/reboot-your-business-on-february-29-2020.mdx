Reboot Your Business: A training workshop on the changes and innovations in the 2020 Finance Law of Cameroon

More about the upcoming [training workshop on the changes and innovations in the 2020 Finance Law](https://openhubdigital.com/training-workshop-changes-in-the-2020-finance-law-of-cameroon/)

The updates keep coming. You need to be part of this as we all want to do business as never before. It’s going to be rich in content and provided by experts in the field. In fact, if you are a business owner, entrepreneur, manager, investor and so on, you need to be part of it.

After consultation with our team and those who have shown their interest to be part of this training workshop, we came up with the following:

### Date, Time: 29 February 2020, 8am - 6pm

#### Why did we choose this day?

\- It’s the end of the month and many will be able to afford the training (especially those who are employees).

\- It’s some few days before March 15 - the last day for declaring your statistics and tax returns (STR). Time for you to analyze and control your STR file for errors and updates.

\- It’s the second month of the year, a good time to put your accounting documents in order considering the changes found in the 2020 Finance Law.

### The Team

Our team is made up of experts (tax & legal advisers, business coaches & tax experts). We are talking here about people who actually know and practice what they will be sharing with you.

### Reboot your business

Below are some of the things we are going to be benefiting from this training:

- Assessment and implementation of common tax measures of interest to businesses and individuals;
- Understand the difficulties inherent in these measures;
- Anticipate expected changes in tax matters;
- Control the legal, tax and social risks linked to the activities of your business
- Understanding the most common legal structures of businesses in Cameroon and what is needed to register one
- Basics in shares and contributions in limited companies

### How it’s going to be done

The team will 

- Provide explanations and examples on daily management;
- Case studies;
- Brainstorming sessions

### Participants

We are calling on managers, accountants, business owners, entrepreneurs and every other person who may be interested to take part in this training session. The graphics will be coming in the days ahead. You will get to know the location and price (very affordable).
