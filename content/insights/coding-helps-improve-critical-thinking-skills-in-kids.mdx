Those who code utilizes the same conventional critical thinking process. They must try, make mistakes, trying again until a solution is found. Learning how to code teaches kids how to think.

By enrolling in Coding4Kids by 3ConeX, your kids will be learning the same steps or process of critical thinking. Coding gives your kids the chance to make mistakes and correct them. This process helps them to think critically.

In our last post, we talked about how [coding can help improve your child’s problem-solving skills](https://openhubdigital.com/coding-teaches-kids-problem-solving-skill/). They learn how to use maths to solve problems in a creative and logical way, as well as improve their thinking skills.

## What is Critical Thinking?

Simply put, critical thinking is all about objectively analysing facts to take a particular course of action. With critical thinking, before you choose a certain action or belief, you must have carefully taken consideration of the available data.

Critical thinking helps for better decision-making, which is not only helpful for programmers, but will be helpful for your children’s future needs. It will help the kids to be able to take a close look at the information they have and use it for logical decision-making.

Our trainers ensure that when a task is given to the children, the methodical approach they will take include:

- Asking questions
- Find and do away with false assumptions
- Evaluate various approaches to the problem and
- Take a decision on the best way to go forward

As I said above, learning to code will teach your children how to think. Programming is not just about kids learning how to type lines of code. It teaches them not only how to think, but to think differently.

For them to code well, they need to use logical thinking. Our trainers will teach them how to break down a large problem into smaller pieces to solve it effectively. They use tools that will help your kid to use their [creativity](https://openhubdigital.com/coding-teaches-kids-problem-solving-skill/) by turning a vague idea to something effective.
