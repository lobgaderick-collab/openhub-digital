**Job Title: Service Engineer**

**Location:** Douala  
**Company:** Alfa Marine Electronics

**About Us:**  
Alfa Marine Electronics is a leading provider of inspection, repair, and maintenance services for navigation and communication equipment on marine vessels. We offer comprehensive support to our clients and are dedicated to excellence in the marine industry.

**Job Description:**  
We have an exciting opportunity for an ambitious Service Engineer to make a real difference through their technical abilities. As a Service Engineer at Alfa Marine Electronics, you will lead and manage various critical tasks, including:

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Diagnose and repair electrical systems, navigation, and communication systems, and other automation systems on marine vessels.

- Conduct thorough inspections to identify and troubleshoot issues.

- Collaborate with team members to ensure timely and efficient repairs and maintenance of systems.

- Maintain accurate records of all maintenance and repairs performed.

- Install, commission, test, and repair marine equipment and accessories.

- Stay updated on industry trends, new technologies, and advancements in the marine industry.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

- Proven experience as a Marine Technician.

- Strong knowledge of marine systems.

- Degree in Electronics Engineering or certification in marine technology is preferred.

- Strong attention to detail and a commitment to quality workmanship.

- Excellent English writing and speaking skills; preference will be given to Anglophones.

- Proficiency in Microsoft Office (MS Excel, Outlook, ERP).

**How to Apply:**  
Interested candidates should fill this [Google Form](https://forms.gle/seRodDwFBh8HiSKx8) \[https://forms.gle/seRodDwFBh8HiSKx8\] or send their resume to hrdesk@alfamarine.com with the position title "Service Engineer" in the subject line.

Join Alfa Marine Electronics and contribute to our mission of providing top-notch service and support in the marine industry!
