Obtaining a Taxpayer Identification in Cameroon is a crucial step for businesses and individuals to comply with tax regulations. This guide outlines the process to register and acquire your Unique Identification Number (NIU) step-by-step.

* * *

### What is a Taxpayer Identification?

A Taxpayer Identification is an official certificate issued by the General Directorate of Taxes (DGI) in Cameroon. It contains your unique **Numéro d’Identifiant Unique (NIU)**, which is used to track your tax obligations.

* * *

### Why Do You Need a Taxpayer Identification?

- **Legal Compliance**: It is mandatory for all businesses and individuals subject to taxation.

- **Official Transactions**: Required for opening bank accounts, bidding for government contracts, and more.

- **Tax Payment**: Enables you to fulfill your tax obligations efficiently.

* * *

### Steps to Register and Obtain Your Taxpayer Identification

#### 1\. Access the Registration Portal

- Visit the official registration portal at [teledeclaration-dgi.cm](https://teledeclaration-dgi.cm/modules/common/Account/Login.aspx).

- Click on **"Je ne suis pas encore immatriculé(e)"** (I am not yet registered).

#### 2\. Choose Your Registration Category

You will be redirected to the registration page: [Registration Page](https://teledeclaration-dgi.cm/modules/common/Account/eregistration.aspx?er=new).

- Select one of the following options:
    - **"Je suis une personne physique"** (I am an individual).
    
    - **"Je suis une personne morale (Société, Organisme...)"** (I am a legal entity, such as a company or organization).

- Tick the box to certify: **"Je certifie avoir pris connaissance des dispositions légales ci-dessus"** (I certify that I have read the legal provisions above).

#### 3\. Fill Out the Registration Form

- Complete the form with accurate information:
    - Personal details for individuals (e.g., name, date of birth, address).
    
    - Business details for companies (e.g., name, registration number, address).

- Submit the form online.

#### 4\. Log In to Your Account

- After submitting the form, log in to your teledeclaration account using your credentials.

#### 5\. Pay the Digitalization Fee

- Follow the steps outlined in our guide: How to Pay the Digitalization Fee for Taxpayer Identification Using teledeclaration-dgi.cm.

- Ensure the payment is completed to access your Taxpayer Identification document.

#### 6\. Download Your Unique Identification Number Certificate

- Once the payment is processed, navigate to the **"Documents"** section in your account.

- Download and save your Unique Identification Number certificate.

* * *

### Additional Notes

- Ensure all information entered is accurate to avoid processing delays.

- For businesses, ensure the company’s legal representative completes the registration.

- Contact the DGI support center at **8200** for assistance or visit your local tax office.
