Starting a reselling business in Cameroon can be a smart way to earn a steady income. The key to success in this venture is finding affordable items that are in high demand. Here’s a list of cheap things you can buy and resell in Cameroon:

# **1\. Phone Accessories**

Phone accessories like cases, chargers, earphones, and screen protectors are always in demand. You can buy these items in bulk at low prices and resell them for a profit. With the increasing use of smartphones, there's a consistent market for these products. See more on [phone accessories business](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-start-a-phone-accessories-reselling-business-in-cameroon/).

# **2\. Fashion Jewelry**

Costume jewelry, including rings, earrings, necklaces, and bracelets, is popular among young people and women who want to look stylish without spending too much. You can find these items at low prices from wholesalers or local markets and resell them at a higher price. See [how you can start one now](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-start-a-fashion-jewelry-reselling-business-in-cameroon/)!

# **3\. Second-Hand Clothes (Okrika)**

Second-hand clothing, or "Okrika," is widely sought after in Cameroon. You can buy bales of these clothes from wholesalers and sell them individually at a profit. Many people prefer Okrika because it’s affordable and often of good quality. See also [How to Start a Second-Hand Clothes Reselling Business in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-start-a-second-hand-clothes-reselling-business-in-cameroon/)

# **4\. Beauty Products**

Affordable beauty products like creams, lotions, and hair care items are also great for reselling. These products have a steady market, especially among women, and can be sourced cheaply in bulk. Read [How to Start a Beauty Products Reselling Business in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/beauty-products-reselling-business-beauty-market-cameroon-skincare-haircare-makeup/) for more.

# **5\. Household Items**

Everyday household items like plastic containers, kitchen gadgets, and cleaning supplies are necessities for many families. You can buy these items in bulk at low prices and resell them in local markets or directly to consumers. Do you want to know more on this? Read [How to Start a Household Items Reselling Business in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-start-a-household-items-reselling-business-in-cameroon/).

# **6\. Small Electronics**

Small electronics like rechargeable lamps, power banks, and Bluetooth speakers are popular in Cameroon, especially in areas where power supply can be unreliable. These items are cheap to buy in bulk and can be sold at a good margin. This blog post on [How to Start a Small Electronics Reselling Business in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-start-a-small-electronics-reselling-business-in-cameroon/) will help you!

# **7\. Stationery**

Items like pens, notebooks, and other school supplies are always in demand, especially during the school year. These are low-cost items that can easily be resold in schools, offices, and local shops.

Selling packaged snacks, bottled water, and soft drinks is a quick way to make money, especially in busy areas like schools, markets, and offices. These items are inexpensive to buy and easy to sell.

Affordable baby clothes, toys, and accessories can be bought at low prices and resold to parents who are looking for good deals. These products are always in demand as parents are constantly shopping for their children.
