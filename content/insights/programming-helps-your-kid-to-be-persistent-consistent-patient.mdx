### Consistency

### Patience

Patience is essential in development for children and encourages them to persevere when learning something new. Children easily get bored, and this has always been a problem in their development.

Programming can help your child become more patient as well as improve [problem-solving](https://openhubdigital.com/coding-teaches-kids-problem-solving-skill/), [critical thinking](https://openhubdigital.com/coding-helps-improve-critical-thinking-skills-in-kids/) and [creative thinking](https://openhubdigital.com/coding-improves-your-childs-creativity-arts-skills/) skills. Patience is an important skill that develops over time.
