This may be that opportunity for you to invest in a successful furniture or woodwork business.

### Fiscal measures to support the furniture sector

The government, in the 2021 Finance Law, has put in place some fiscal measures to boost this sector.

- Imported capital goods (machinery, apparatus and equipment) used for forestry or in the transformation of wood have been exempted from taxes and customs duties.
- An excise duty of 25% has been imposed on wooden articles, furniture and toothpick.
- [A reduced corporate tax rate of 28% for domestic companies](https://openhubdigital.com/corporate-tax-rates-to-be-applied-in-cameroon/?&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=name_what_about_starting_a_successful_furniture_business&utm_term=2021-01-20)
