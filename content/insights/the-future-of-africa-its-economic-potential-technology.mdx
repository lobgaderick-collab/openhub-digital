**The future of Africa and its economic potential or prosperity will highly be dependent on technology.**

It is expected that by 2025, about 40% of sub-Saharan Africa will be using mobile internet subscriptions – _“Mobile Economy Sub-Saharan Africa 2019”, a report by the GSMA._

This is to tell you that Information & Communication Technology will influence engineering, science, business, politics, law, culture, and many other areas in the life of Africans.

### Prepare Now!

There’s no time like the present for you to prepare this future for generations to come by introducing the use of digital technology to your kids.

Together, let’s build a new tech generation of kids who will become experts in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cloud Experts, and more.

To achieve this, we have to give our kids the [opportunity to learn more and develop their technical skills](https://openhubdigital.com/coding-teaches-kids-problem-solving-skill/) from a very young age.

This will help them prepare for innovation, and future careers, build a better community, and encourage engagement and imagination.

It will also help them play a more active role in the classroom and education in general. Introducing kids to technology helps them to easily carry out tasks, and develop mathematical skills and [logical reasoning](https://openhubdigital.com/coding-helps-improve-critical-thinking-skills-in-kids/).

For Africa to develop a successful digital economy, we’ll need the right training and mindset to build and manage the digital infrastructure. Now is the time to start!

### Introduce Your Kids to Technology

To play its part in this digital revolution, TriConeX Ltd (3ConeX), an IT Startup based in Douala, Cameroon, is providing children from the age of 7 to 17 with the opportunity to learn and build skills in coding at an early age. This will go a long way to enable them to leave a mark on the world.

They offer courses that aim at increasing information and technology literacy for the younger generation by introducing them to the basics of Computer Science, teaching them how to code with the simplest language to more advanced methods and programming languages.

With “[Coding for Kids](https://3conex.com/our-story/)”, 3ConeX has the vision to equip the _“young generation with a bright tech future and build up young entrepreneurs who will be experts in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, and Data Science.”_

Giving your kids the chance to code their favorite games at an early age will make learning fun and more engaging.
