### What Does It Mean to Be an Independent Salesperson?

Selling is a very personal thing. You are probably sensing that now. Each sales agent has his or her own personality, style, and approach to selling.

An independent salesperson is someone who sells products or services for a company without being employed by that company. Instead, they work as a contractor, selling products or services on their own behalf.

It’s a win-win situation, with the sales agent earning a commission for every successful sale and the purchaser getting the best deal possible.

And, the sales agent doesn’t have to do anything to make a sale. He or she just has to be a good salesperson.
