Opening a bank account with UBA Cameroon is not a formality. It is a structured onboarding process based on clearly defined documentation requirements.

This guide presents only the document lists officially published by UBA Cameroon and explains how to prepare your file properly.

### Why opening a UBA bank account in Cameroon requires detailed documentation

UBA must be able to confirm:

- the legal existence of the customer,

- the ownership structure,

- the authorised signatories, and

- the physical location of the activity.

This is part of its internal control and regulatory obligations.

### UBA Cameroon – documents required to open a resident company account (SA / SARL)

#### Legal and registration documents

- Company Incorporation Certificate (RCCM)

- Articles of association (OHADA), notarised or under private seal, authenticated by administrative representatives

- Licence or specific authorisation to operate certain activities

- Minutes of the Annual General Meeting or Board of Directors appointing officers, if not provided for in the by-laws

### Shareholders and management identification

- Copy of a valid identity document of the shareholder or majority shareholder

- Company Registration Certificate if the majority shareholder is a company

- Copy of the national identity card of the statutory manager (SARL) or managing director (SA), and other signatories if applicable

### Administrative and operational documents

- Unique Identification Number (NIU)

- Two passport-size photographs and copy of valid ID of the company representative(s)

- Signatory powers and operating procedures for the account

- Location map of the head office or water or electricity bill

- Financial report or last annual report certified by the statutory auditor, if required

- Signed request to open an account on company letterhead

### UBA Cameroon – documents required to open a non-resident company account

- Notarised extract from the company trade register

- Notarised articles of association

- Approval or specific authorisation to operate certain activities

- Minutes appointing officers, if not provided for in the articles

- Unique Identification Number (NIU)

- Power of attorney of the legal representative, certified by a notary public

- Two passport-size photographs and copy of valid ID of the legal representative

- Copy of valid ID of the shareholder or majority shareholder

- Company Incorporation Certificate (RCCM) if the majority shareholder is a company

- Localisation plan of the head office

- Financial report for the previous year, if required

- Letter of good standing from current bankers

### UBA Cameroon – documents required to open an association account

### Religious associations

- Authorisation by decree of the President of the Republic

#### Sports associations and federations

- Approval from the Minister of Sports

#### Other associations

- Final receipt from the Territorial Administration or proof of registration (less than two months old)

- Signed application to open an account

- Association by-laws

- Complete list of members

- Minutes of the General Meeting authorising the opening of the account with UBA Cameroon and designating signatories

- Attendance list

- Copies of valid identity documents and localisation plans of signatories’ homes

- Location map of the association’s offices

- Signatory powers and operating procedures

- Unique Identification Number

### UBA Cameroon – documents required to open an individual business account

- Registration Certificate (RCCM), except liberal professions

- Approval or authorisation to exercise certain activities

- Attestation of non-redevance

- Taxpayer card or NIU certificate

- Copy of valid identity document of the promoter or account holder

- Signatory powers and modalities of account operation

- Two passport-size photographs per signatory

- Location plan of the establishment

- Unique Identification Number

### UBA Cameroon – documents required to open a personal bank account

### Resident

- Copy of valid national identity card

- Proof of professional activity or income

- Two passport-size photographs per signatory

- House location map or utility bill

- Unique Identification Number

### Non-resident

- Copy of residence permit or residence card and passport, where applicable

- Proof of professional activity or income

- Letter of good standing from the usual bank

- Two passport-size photographs per holder

- Proof of address or utility bill

- Unique Identification Number

## What usually delays UBA files

Most UBA files are delayed because internal documents do not align.

UBA cross-checks:

- RCCM details,

- statutes,

- meeting minutes, and

- signature mandates.

If there is inconsistency, the file is escalated for verification.
