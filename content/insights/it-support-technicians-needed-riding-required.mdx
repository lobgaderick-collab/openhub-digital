IT Support Technicians Recruitment Opportunity

A telecommunications company is seeking fresh IT talent for Douala and Yaounde roles.

The company is looking to expand their team with young professionals aged 23-30 who have:

- Degree or background in Computer Science, IT, programming or related fields

- Strong oral and written skills in English and French

- Analytical and problem-solving mindset

- Comfortable riding bicycles for transportation between worksites

You will work both independently and collaboratively on innovative projects that challenge and fulfill.

Successful candidates will receive full training before being placed on-site with clients to provide technical support, installations, and troubleshooting services. Success in this role demands mobility - so candidates should ride bikes to various client sites around the city.

Bike-savvy IT professionals can apply now by emailing their CVs via email [sales@openhubconsulting.com](mailto:sales@openhubconsulting.com).
