A Training Workshop on the changes and innovation of the 2019 Finance Law Cameroon will be organized by **Grant Thornton Tax and Legal** in Douala on **January 24, 2019** at **La Falaise Hotel in Bonanjo, Douala**. The training that will begin at **7:30am and end at 6:30pm** will drill participants on how the 2019 finance law will impact tax and operational aspects of businesses.

The training workshop will also take a look at the 2018 finance law whose putting in place has significantly affected the 2018 financial statements. It should be noted that businesses are required to declare their tax returns in Cameroon for the previous financial year not later than March 15 each year. So this training workshop will be of great help to participants as they prepare to declare the 2018 income tax returns of their various companies.

The workshop by **Grant Thornton Cameroun** will draw participants from various walks of life including Accountants, Auditors, Bankers, Tax Accountants, Finance Managers, Managers, Business owners and other interested persons.

#### Why you need to attend the Workshop on 2019 Finance Law Cameroon

The **2019 Finance Law of Cameroon Training Workshop** organized by Grant Thornton Tax and Legal is out to give professionals, specialists and business owners focus on the following:

- How to appreciate and apply the tax changes and innovations that will affect companies and individuals
    
- To understand the difficulties inherent in the changes put in place by the 2019 Finance Law of Cameroon
    
- Have a summary of the principal changes affecting businesses and individuals
    
- Anticipate on expected developments in tax issues
    
- Master professional, legal, tax and social risks related to maritime and port activities

Participants are expected to work in groups in other to brainstorm, carry out case studies with concrete examples.

The workshop will be divided into three modules:

**Module 1:** Common changes that affects all sectors

**Module 2:** Specific changes and innovations affecting certain enterprises in a sector

**Module 3:** Changes related to the **Manual of Tax Procedures**

**Module 4:** Changes and innovations related to custom duties and dues

All modules will be separated by short coffee breaks and lunch after the Module 2. Speakers at the workshop will include two **CEMAC certified tax advisors** and a **seasoned tax inspector**.

Those to take part in this training workshop include:

- General managers or Managers
    
- Employees of the Department of Administration & Finance
    
- Employees of the Human Resources Department
    
- Accountants, auditors and others in the accounting department
    
- Every other interested persons
