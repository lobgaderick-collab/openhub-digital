### What Incomes Are Exempt from Personal Income Tax?

Section 43 provides several key exemptions from personal income tax. These exemptions are designed to alleviate the tax burden on specific types of interest and small capital gains. Let’s explore these exemptions in detail:

#### 1\. **Interest on State and Local Authority Loans**

Interest earned from negotiable securities related to loans issued by the State or regional and local authorities is exempt from personal income tax. This encourages investment in government bonds and securities.

#### Example

If you hold a government bond issued by the Cameroonian State and earn CFAF 200,000 in interest, this interest is exempt from personal income tax.

#### 2\. **Interest on Savings Accounts**

Interest accruing on savings accounts with deposits not exceeding CFAF 50 million is exempt. This supports individual savings and provides tax relief on smaller deposits.

#### Example

Suppose you have a savings account with a balance of CFAF 45 million. The interest earned, say CFAF 300,000, is exempt from personal income tax as it falls below the CFAF 50 million threshold.

#### 3\. **Interest on Housing Savings Accounts**

Interest on savings accounts specifically designated for housing purposes is exempt. This promotes saving for housing and related investments.

#### Example

Interest earned on a dedicated housing savings account, regardless of the amount, is exempt from personal income tax, aiding in homeownership savings.

#### 4\. **Interest on Cash Vouchers**

Interest on cash vouchers is exempt. Cash vouchers are a form of short-term debt instrument, and the exemption encourages their use.

#### Example

If you hold cash vouchers that yield CFAF 100,000 in interest, this amount is exempt from personal income tax.

#### 5\. **Small Capital Gains**

Net overall capital gains not exceeding CFAF 500,000 are exempt. This provides relief on smaller gains from the transfer of stocks, bonds, or other capital shares.

#### Example

If you sell shares and realize a profit of CFAF 400,000, this gain is exempt from personal income tax. However, if the gain is CFAF 600,000, you would be taxed on the entire amount.

### Why Are These Exemptions Important?

Understanding these exemptions helps you manage your investments and savings more effectively, ensuring you maximize your tax benefits. This is particularly valuable for individuals and businesses seeking to optimize their tax liabilities.

### Key Points to Remember

- Interest on government and local authority loans is exempt.

- Savings interest below CFAF 50 million is exempt.

- Housing savings interest is exempt.

- Interest on cash vouchers is exempt.

- Small capital gains below CFAF 500,000 are exempt.
