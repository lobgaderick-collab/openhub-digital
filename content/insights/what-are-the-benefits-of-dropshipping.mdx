### No need for inventory

If you ask my opinion, this should be one of the biggest benefits of dropshipping. It is also one of the reason I am writing about it. That is, to share businesses that you can start with very little funds.

The dropshipper doesn’t need to carry any inventory. This makes it less risky and less work for the owner. Very little costs is incurred as you don’t need to spend on a warehouse to hold stock.

### Low startup cost

As said above, dropshipping business is a low cost business model. This are the reasons:

- you don’t need a warehouse to stock goods
- you don’t even need to purchase the products in the first place except a customer places an order and
- you don’t need to hire someone to manage the product shipping. The only expenses will be the cost of goods (in case you have to pay before), advertising and website (in the case where you own your website). With Jumia Seller Center, the last two are taken care of for the dropshipper.

### Dropshipping saves sellers time

One of the best benefits of dropshipping is the fact that it is a great time-saver. The dropshipper spends more time marketing products since billing and shipping are handled by the supplier.

### Flexibility

You can drop a product at any time if it is not performing well. Just compare this with the case where you have bought stock in advance. You may end up stocking or throwing away goods that doesn’t perform well in the market. One of the benefits of dropshipping is that you can offer a variety of products in large numbers without worrying about storage or financial outlay.
