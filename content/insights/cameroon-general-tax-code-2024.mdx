**About the General Tax Code:**

The General Tax Code of Cameroon serves as a comprehensive guide to understanding the tax laws and regulations in Cameroon. It outlines the various taxes applicable to individuals, businesses, and other entities, providing clarity on their obligations and responsibilities.
