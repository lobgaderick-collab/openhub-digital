The prices for various commodities (cocoa and coffee) in Cameroon were as follows:

### September 25, 2023

The current prices of cocoa and coffee in Cameroon are as follows:

## Cocoa

- CAF LON: 2322 FCFA/KG

- FOB DLA: 2257 FCFA/KG

- Purchase price in Douala:

- MIN: 1600 FCFA/KG

- MAX: 1700 FCFA/KG

## Robusta Coffee

- CAF: 1528 FCFA/KG

- FOB: 1433 FCFA/KG

- Purchase price in Moungo:

- MIN: 1150 FCFA/KG

- MAX: 1300 FCFA/KG

## Arabica Coffee

- CAF: 2053 FCFA/KG

- FOB: 1946 FCFA/KG

These prices are provided by the National Cocoa and Coffee Board (ONCC/NCCB) and are subject to market conditions. The purchase prices may vary in different regions depending on local supply and demand.

It is important to note that these prices are indicated in FCFA (Franc CFA), the currency used in Cameroon and other countries in the West and Central Africa region.

For more information on cocoa and coffee prices in Cameroon, you can visit the official website of ONCC/NCCB at [https://oncc.cm/prices](https://oncc.cm/prices).

### September 20, 2023

On September 20, 2023, the prices for cocoa and robusta in Cameroon were as follows:

Cocoa:

- CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight): 2289 FCFA/kg

- FOB (Free on Board): 2225 FCFA/kg

- Buying Price in Douala, by Exporters:
    - MINI: 1600 FCFA/kg
    
    - MAXI: 1700 FCFA/kg

Robusta Coffee:

- CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight): 1646 FCFA/kg

- FOB (Free on Board): 1549 FCFA/kg

- Buying Price in Moungo:
    - MINI: 1150 FCFA/kg
    
    - MAXI: 1300 FCFA/kg

These prices reflect the market rates for these commodities during that period in Cameroon. It's important to note that commodity prices can fluctuate due to various factors such as supply and demand, global market conditions, and other economic factors.
