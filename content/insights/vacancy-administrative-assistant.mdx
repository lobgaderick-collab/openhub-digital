**Job Title:** Administrative Assistant (Full-Time)  
**Location:** Bonamoussadi, Douala  
**Company:** OpenHub Consulting

**Role Overview:**  
OpenHub Consulting is seeking an organized and proactive **Full-Time Administrative Assistant** to support our daily operations. The ideal candidate must reside in or around **Bonamoussadi** or within a **taxi drop distance** for convenience.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Manage schedules, appointments.

- Answer calls, emails.

- Organize files, prepare reports, and assist with meetings.

- Maintain office supplies and handle administrative tasks.

**Qualifications:**

- Proven administrative experience.

- Proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Outlook).

- Strong organizational and communication skills.

- Must live in or near **Bonamoussadi** or within a **taxi drop distance**.

- High school diploma or equivalent; certifications a plus.

- Preferably English-speaking, but knowledge in French is a plus.

**Why Join Us?**

- Competitive salary and benefits.

- Supportive and inclusive work environment.

- Opportunities for professional growth.

**How to Apply:**  
Send your resume to **[vacancy@openhubconsulting.com](https://mailto:vacancy@openhubconsulting.com/)** by **March 14, 2025**.

OpenHub Consulting is an equal opportunity employer.
