The Ministry of Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises, Social Economy, and Handicrafts has announced the 8th edition of the National Technology Day, _Journées Technologiques National_es - JTN. Scheduled from October 23 to 25, 2024, in Douala, this event aims to promote innovation and local expertise.

Participants will include Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs), Social Economy Organisations (SEO), Craft Production Units (CPU), and innovators. The JTN focuses on sectors such as mining, agri-food, digital technology, energy, health and wellness, mobility, environment, security, housing, leisure, and culture.

The event will provide a platform for showcasing products and innovations. It will also equip local promoters with strategic skills in industrial property management. Additionally, it will identify top products and innovations for prototyping support and facilitate B2B meetings with potential investors.

Interested parties are invited to submit their applications through the regional and departmental delegations of the Ministry of Mines, Industry, and Technological Development (MINMIDT). Application forms are available at these delegations and online at [www.minmidt.cm](http://www.minmidt.cm).

It is worth noting that this event offers significant opportunities for local businesses and innovators. By connecting with investors and industry leaders, participants can enhance their visibility and strategic positioning.

The JTN, according to the Minister, is an initiative in advancing Cameroon's technological and industrial landscape. It aligns with national goals of fostering innovation and supporting economic growth.

For further details and registration, applicants should visit the [MINMIDT website](http://www.minmidt.cm) or contact their local delegations. This event promises to be a landmark occasion for driving technological advancements and supporting entrepreneurial initiatives in Cameroon.
