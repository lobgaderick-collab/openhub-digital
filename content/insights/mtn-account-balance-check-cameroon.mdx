## How to Access Your Account Balance with MTN

Keeping track of your account balance is easy with MTN. Simply use the following codes:

- **Account Balance:** Dial [\*155#](tel:*155#)
- **Internet Balance:** Dial [\*157\*99#](tel:*157*99#)
