The snacks and drinks reselling business is thriving in Cameroon, especially in urban areas where there is a constant demand for refreshments. This venture can be highly profitable if approached correctly. Here’s a comprehensive guide to help you start your own snacks and drinks reselling business.

### Steps to Get Started:

1. **Decide Your Target Market**  
    Understand who your primary customers will be—students, workers, or travelers. This will help you select the right types of snacks and drinks to offer. For instance, if you’re located near a school, focus on quick, affordable snacks and beverages that cater to students.

3. **Source Your Products**  
    Buy snacks and drinks from local wholesalers or distributors. For a unique edge, consider sourcing some items from local producers or importing popular foreign snacks that are not widely available in Cameroon.

5. **Choose a Sales Location**  
    Location is key in the snacks and drinks business. Opt for high-traffic areas like markets, bus stops, or schools. Alternatively, you can set up a mobile kiosk or offer home delivery services, which are becoming increasingly popular.

7. **Set Competitive Prices**  
    Price your products competitively to attract a broad customer base while ensuring profitability. Offering combo deals or discounts can also boost sales and draw in more customers.

9. **Market Your Business**  
    Use word of mouth by encouraging satisfied customers to spread the word. Enhance your presence on social media by posting engaging pictures of your products and special offers. Consider implementing referral discounts to incentivize customer recommendations.
