**Consultant Opportunity: Communication Consultant**

**Organization:** Sekakoh  
**Location:** Cameroon  
**Duration:** Estimated 30 days (1 month), including two visits to Sekakoh's office

**Context and Justification:** [Sekakoh](https://sekakoh.org/job-opportunities/) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to environmental conservation operating across four regions (Centre, North, Northwest, Littoral). We are seeking an experienced communication consultant to develop a comprehensive and effective communication plan. This plan aims to:

- Increase visibility of our organization and actions

- Raise public awareness about environmental issues

- Mobilize community support

- Recruit new members and volunteers

- Raise funds for our projects

**Mission Objectives:** The consultant will be tasked with:

- Conducting a communication audit of the organization in consultation with management.

- Elaborately a complete, detailed and up to date communication plan for Sekakoh

**Expected Deliverables:** Upon completion of the mission, Sekakoh expects to have a comprehensive and effective communication plan that meets its communication objectives.

**Consultant Profile:** The communication expert should possess the following qualifications:

- Minimum of 5 years of experience in communication

- In-depth knowledge of environmental issues

- Excellent writing and analytical skills

- Strong interpersonal and persuasive abilities

- Adaptability and autonomy

**Mission Duration and Workflow:**

- Estimated duration: 30 days (1 month)

- Two visits to Sekakoh's office:
    - The first visit to gather necessary information for the task
    
    - The second visit including a 2-day presentation of the comprehensive communication plan to Sekakoh's team for validation

**How to Apply:** Interested consultants should submit their CVs and relevant details to lefordenis@yahoo.fr and info@sekakoh.com by **15/07/2024**.

**Selection Process:** Shortlisted applicants will be contacted for further arrangements.
