As the end of the year approaches, it is essential for taxpayers in Cameroon to be aware of important information regarding their tax obligations. In this article, we will discuss the key aspects of end of year tax filing, including the determination of taxable profits, deductions, assessment periods, applicable tax rates, and the deadline for filing taxes.

### Determination of Taxable Profits

Profits liable to company tax in Cameroon are determined solely based on profits earned by businesses carried out or transactions conducted within the country, subject to the provisions of international conventions. This means that only profits generated from activities within Cameroon are taken into account for tax purposes.

### Deductions for Net Taxable Profit

Net taxable profit is established by deducting all charges directly related to the exercise of activities subject to assessment in Cameroon. These deductions include expenses incurred in carrying out business activities, such as operating costs, employee salaries, rent, utilities, and other relevant expenses.

### Assessment Period and Balance Sheet

The company tax is assessed based on the profits realized during the twelve-month period corresponding to the financial year. However, for companies that start their business within six months preceding the prescribed closing date, they may draw up their first balance sheet at the end of the financial year following the twelve-month period in which they started activities.

If successive balance sheets are prepared during the same fiscal year, the results of these balance sheets are added together for the assessment of the tax due for the following financial year.

### Applicable Tax Rates

The rate of company tax in Cameroon is generally 30%. However, there is a reduced tax rate of 25% for taxpayers with a turnover equal to or below XAF3 billion. It is important to note that in the calculation of the tax, any fraction of taxable profit less than XAF1,000 shall be disregarded.

### Deadline for Tax Filing

Taxpayers in Cameroon are required to file their taxes on or before March 15, 2024. It is crucial to meet this deadline to avoid any penalties or fines. Filing taxes on time ensures compliance with the tax regulations and helps in maintaining a good standing with the tax authorities.
