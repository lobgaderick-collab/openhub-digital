In Cameroon, the value-added tax (VAT) system operates differently depending on a business's turnover. Under the Actual Earnings Tax System, only companies with a turnover exceeding XAF 50,000,000 are required to collect VAT. For these businesses, invoices include VAT, meaning the tax is added to the total amount due. This is known as VAT-inclusive invoicing.

On the other hand, businesses with a turnover below XAF 50,000,000 operate under the Simplified Taxation Regime and are not required to collect VAT. Invoices for these businesses are VAT-exclusive, meaning the tax is not included in the total amount due.
