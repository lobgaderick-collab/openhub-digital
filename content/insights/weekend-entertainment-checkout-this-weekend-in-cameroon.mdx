### Movie Premiere: Married Singles

This weekend seems to be a movie premiere weekend in Yaounde. Just before you get your man or lady for the weekend in Yaounde, you may have to be a ‘married single’ today.

The new movie, **Married Single** will be premiering in Yaounde today, Friday 3, 2017 at Hilton Hotel. The movie is produced by Ngato Brian and starring top Cameroonian actors like **Candy Laurice, Fonguh Claudia, Elvi Jones, Aisatou Nyali** and many others. It will start with a Red Carpet at 6pm and event proper at 7pm. Check out more [here](https://blog.njokatv.com/2017/10/30/movie-married-singles-premiered-november-3rd/).

### Weekend Soccer: Cameroon

It’s going to be a great weekend for soccer fans in Cameroon. It’s the weekend of the 2017 Cup of Cameroon semi finals. The 2015 cup winner **UMS de Loum** will lock horns with **Yong Sports Academy of Bamenda** that won the cup in 2013 on November 5, 2017 at the Douala Reunification Stadium at 2pm.

\[trend-ad-shortcode id="4512" title="Leaderboard Google Adsense"\]

Meanwhile at the Douala Reunification stadium on November 4, 2017, third division side, **Victoria United of Limbe** looking for a place in the finals with **New Stars of Douala**. It’s not going to be an easy ride for New Stars as Victoria United in the last 14 games recorded 11 wins, one defeat and one draw in the third division league games, finishing first at the regional level with 35 points.
