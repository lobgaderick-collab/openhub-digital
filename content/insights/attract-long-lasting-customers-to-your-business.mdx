What does it take to attract customers to become loyal or fall in love with your business? Keeping long-lasting customers is one of the most important operations of a business.

You carry out business because there are customers. You stay in business because of repeat customers and new customers.

It’s not easy to convert and attract new customers. As well as, it’s not also easy to keep them once they’re converted.

With customers looking for businesses they can buy from, businesses also need to keep the customers engage, understand their needs and build relationships with them.

All focus should be on customer retention – Retention Marketing.
