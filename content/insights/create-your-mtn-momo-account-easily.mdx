## Get Started with MTN MoMo: Your Guide to Creating and Using Your Account

MTN MoMo makes managing your finances and payments easy. Here’s how you can create your MoMo account and start enjoying all its benefits:

### How to Create an Account

Creating your MTN MoMo account is simple. First, make sure you're properly identified, then follow these steps:

1. Dial [\*126#](tel:*126#)
2. Choose your 5-digit MoMo PIN and confirm to complete your registration.

### Can I Have Multiple MoMo Accounts?

Yes, you can have multiple MoMo accounts if you own several phone numbers (up to 3). Simply dial [\*126#](tel:*126#) and select your MoMo PIN for each account.

### How to Use Your MoMo Account

Once your account is set up, you can start using MoMo by dialing [\*126#](tel:*126#) and accessing the MoMo Menu. Here, you can:

- Send money to friends and family
- Pay bills like ENEO or MTN bills
- Purchase MTN products or services

MTN MoMo makes it easier than ever to manage your daily transactions, all from your mobile phone.
