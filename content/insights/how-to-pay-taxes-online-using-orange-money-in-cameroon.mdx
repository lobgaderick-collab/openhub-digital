Paying taxes in Cameroon has been simplified with the partnership between Orange Money and the General Directorate of Taxes (DGI). This guide explains how to pay taxes online using the USSD code and details the associated fees.

* * *

### Taxes and Duties Payable via Orange Money

Using Orange Money, you can pay the following taxes and duties:

- **Personal Income Tax**

- **Corporate Income Tax**

- **Stamp Duties**

- **Property Taxes**

The amounts and specific rates for each tax category are available on the DGI tax portal.

* * *

### Steps to Pay Taxes Online with Orange Money

#### 1\. Dial the USSD Code

- Dial **#150\***_**3**_**\*4#** on your mobile device.

#### 2\. Select the Tax Payment Option

- Choose **Option 2 (Paiement des impôts)**.

#### 3\. If Your NIU is Pre-registered

- Choose **Option 1 (Mobile Tax)**.

- Enter your **Numéro d’Identifiant Unique (NIU)**.

- Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the payment.

#### 4\. If Your NIU is Not Pre-registered

- After entering **#150**_\***3**_**\*4\*2#**, choose **Option 2 (Autres déclarations)**.

- Enter the declaration number obtained from the DGI portal.

> **Note**: Always declare your taxes on the DGI platform before proceeding to payment.

#### 5\. Enter the Payment Amount

- Input the exact amount of tax due as specified by the DGI.

- Double-check the amount to avoid errors.

#### 6\. Confirm the Payment

- Verify the payment details, including your NIU and the tax amount.

- Authorize the payment by entering your Orange Money PIN.

#### 7\. Receive a Confirmation

- Upon successful payment, you will receive an SMS confirmation from Orange Money.

- Additionally, log into the DGI portal to download and print your official payment receipt.
