The [International Conference on Sustainable Energy and Climate in African Municipalities](https://www.secam-conference.org/) (SECAM) will be held on May 22 – 25, 2018. The conference, which is the first of its kind will take place at the Ecole National Superieure des Travaux Publics (ENSTP), Yaounde, Cameroon. The theme for this year’s conference will be _**“Accelerating African Municipality’s Potential towards Sustainable Development Goals”**_.

This year’s forum will be dedicated to the Sustainable Development Goals 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy), 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities), 12 (Responsible Production and Consumption) and 13 (Climate Action) in African municipalities.

![SECAM 2018](images/sdgs-sustainable-development-goals.jpg)

The topics for SECAM 2018 will include waste management, green transport, clean cooking, heating and indoor air quality, business and entrepreneurship among others. [_Check out full list of topics_](https://www.secam-conference.org/topics).

The conference will be chaired by Professor Nkeng George Elambo, Director of the ENSTP.According to him, this conference which is the first of it’s kind in Africa _“should reflect and tackle the concerns of the African municipalities including the built environment industry and create an action plan for change.”_
