### New Filing Deadlines According to the 2023 Finance Law

The 2023 Finance Law has brought about changes in the filing deadlines. It is essential to be aware of these dates to avoid any penalties or complications. The new deadlines are as follows:

- **March 15:** Deadline for taxpayers falling under the large-scale enterprises category.

- **April 15:** Deadline for taxpayers under the medium-size tax centers and specialized tax centers categories.

- **May 15:** Deadline for taxpayers under the divisional taxation centers category.
