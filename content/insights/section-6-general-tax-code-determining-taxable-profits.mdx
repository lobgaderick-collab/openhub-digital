Section 6 of the General Tax Code elaborates on the method for calculating taxable profits. This section ensures that businesses accurately report their income to adhere to Cameroon’s tax laws.

### **Key Provisions for Taxable Profits**

1. **Scope of Taxable Profits**:
    - Taxable profits include income from all transactions carried out during the financial year, like:
        
        - Profits from business operations.
        
        - Gains from the transfer of assets.

3. **Definition of Net Profits**:
    - Net profit is calculated as the difference between the closing and opening net assets for the financial year. Adjustments are made for contributions and withdrawals by members during this period.

5. **Valuation of Assets**:
    - **Stocks**:
        - Valued at cost price. If the market value is lower than the cost price, depreciation provisions must be made.
    
    - **Work in Progress**:
        - Valued at production cost, ensuring accurate reflection of ongoing projects.

7. **Expenses and Deductions**:
    - Only documented and justified expenses are deductible.
    
    - **Reminder**: As emphasized in Section 8a, expenses supported by invoices lacking mandatory information are non-deductible. Invoices issued outside the tax authority’s electronic invoicing system are also non-deductible.
