**About KidzHub**

KidzHub English Nursery and Primary School is a new educational institution located in Bonamoussadi, Douala. We offer an international-standard education that blends the strengths of both the British and Cameroonian curricula, creating a hybrid programme designed to equip children with a strong academic foundation, critical thinking skills, and global perspectives while remaining rooted in local relevance and values.

### **Join Our Team**

Working with us is more than just a job – it is an opportunity to shape young minds, gain valuable experience, and be part of a dynamic team dedicated to excellence.
