![](images/app-development-300x300.jpg)Successful entrepreneurs are informed entrepreneurs. Entrepreneurs need to be informed about entrepreneurial activities taking place around them. This will help them mold their lives as future leaders. There are many activities taking place (both paid and free) that budding entrepreneurs can benefit from. The problem is that most of it is not well publicized or people don’t search or go for it.

To sort out this problem, OpenHub.site has decided to publish monthly events organized for entrepreneurs or students. These events can help them in their journey as entrepreneurs. Check out this space frequently for updates.

Event organizers should also get in touch with us in other to publish their events.

### November Events Calendar for Entrepreneurs

Microsoft Certified Solutions Associate Training Boot-camp (Free)

The boot-camp is organized by Ngueti IT Solutions and will take place on November 15, 2017 at Ngueti IT Solutions Bepanda, Douala.

\[trend-ad-shortcode id="4512" title="Leaderboard Google Adsense"\]

This is a good opportunity for those who want to get training in Microsoft Technologies. Check out more [here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/microsoft-certified-solutions-associate-training-bootcamp-tickets-39472143243?aff=ehomecard).

Journée des Entrepreneurs – 2017 (Free & Paid)

The second edition of the Journée des Entrepreneurs will take place in Douala on November 16, 2017 at the Sawa Hotel. This annual event is organized for young entrepreneurs who see in them the building of a vibrant Cameroon in the future. The future is now, don’t let this opportunity pass by. Check out more [here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/inscription-journee-des-entrepreneurs2017-36619778744?aff=ehomecard).

Silicon Mountain Conference (SMConf) 2017 (Free)

![](images/silicon-mountain-conference-2017.jpg-300x150.png)This one is not to be missed. The Silicon Mountain Community will be organizing this year’s edition of the Silicon Mountain Conference (SMConf) at the Chariot Hotel in Buea on November 18, 2017 at 8am - 5pm.

\[trend-ad-shortcode id="4512" title="Leaderboard Google Adsense"\]

SMConf brings together techies and developers with the Fako techosystem as well as other developers and tech enthusiasts from Cameroon and out of the country. The event serves as a means to attract potential investors. Make sure you [add this to your calendar](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/silicon-mountain-conference-smconf-2017-tickets-39184951244?aff=ehomefriend).

Entrepreneurship Workshop No 1 (Paid)

This event will take place on November 22, 2017 at Somatel Hotel Bali, Douala. The seminar is for entrepreneurs and budding entrepreneurs. Participants will learn about creating a business plan that will attract investors and how to pitch your business in front of investors or potential partners. Check out more on this training seminar [here](https://www.eventbrite.fr/e/billets-entrepreneurship-workshop-n-1-39676975903?aff=ehomecard).

Les Journées de la Robotique Industrielle (Free) \[trend-ad-shortcode id="4512" title="Leaderboard Google Adsense"\]

This is for those who are interested in industrial robotics. This will take place at the Faculty of Industrial Engineering of the University of Douala on November 25, 2017 at 6pm. The aim of the seminar is to bring together students of industrial robotics and enterprises to have an informed discussion on how they can work together. Check out more [here](https://www.eventbrite.fr/e/billets-les-journees-de-la-robotique-industrielle-38697843291?aff=ehomecard) if you are interested.
