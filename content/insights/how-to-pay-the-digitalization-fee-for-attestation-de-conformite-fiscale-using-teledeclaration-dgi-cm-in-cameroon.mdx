### How to Pay the Digitalization Fee for Attestation de Conformité Fiscale Using teledeclaration-dgi.cm in Cameroon

Obtaining your Attestation de Conformité Fiscale (Tax Compliance Certificate) in Cameroon requires paying the digitalization fee (_Redevance pour la digitalisation de la DGI_). This guide explains the payment process step-by-step.

* * *

### 1\. Access the Platform

Visit the official teledeclaration portal at [teledeclaration-dgi.cm](https://teledeclaration-dgi.cm/).

* * *

### 2\. Log In

- Enter your **Numéro d’Identifiant Unique (NIU)** and password.

- For non-professional users, the default password is `0000`.

* * *

### 3\. Navigate to "Timbre fiscal et redevance"

- After logging in, locate the menu on the left side of the dashboard.

- Click on **"Déclarations"**.

- Select **"Timbre fiscal et redevance"** to manage declarations for the digitalization fee.

* * *

### 4\. Declare the Digitalization Fee for Attestation de Conformité Fiscale

- Within the **"Timbre fiscal et redevance"** section, choose the option for **"Redevance pour la digitalisation"**.

- Provide the required details, including your **NIU**.

- Review and confirm the information entered.

* * *

### 5\. Generate the Payment Notice

- After submitting the declaration, generate the **"Avis d’Imposition"** (Tax Notice).

- This document will detail the amount due and the payment reference.

* * *

### 6\. Choose a Payment Method

The platform offers several payment methods, including:

- **Bank Transfer**: Use the details provided in the **"Avis d’Imposition"** to make a transfer from your bank.

- **Mobile Money**: Payments can be made through services like Orange Money or MTN Mobile Money.

- **Cash Payment**: Pay directly at authorized bank counters.

* * *

### 7\. Complete the Payment

- Follow the instructions specific to your chosen payment method.

- Ensure you use the correct payment reference to facilitate accurate processing.

* * *

### 8\. Obtain Your Attestation de Conformité Fiscale

- After the payment is processed, log back into your teledeclaration account.

- Navigate to the **"Documents"** section to download your Attestation de Conformité Fiscale.

* * *

### Additional Notes

- The digitalization fee ensures secure and efficient online access to tax documents.

- For detailed guidance on using the teledeclaration platform, refer to the [user manual](https://teledeclaration-dgi.cm/UploadedFiles/Formulaires/manuel_contribuable.pdf).

- If you encounter any issues or require further assistance, contact the DGI support center at **8200** or consult your local tax office for support.
