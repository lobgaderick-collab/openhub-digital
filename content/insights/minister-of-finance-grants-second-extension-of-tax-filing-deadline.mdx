Businesses in Cameroon have been granted a second extension on the deadline for filing taxes for the 2022 fiscal year.

The Minister of Finance has granted a moratorium to extend the deadline to April 30, 2023. This exceptional extension is intended to support taxpayers learn how to file records from paper to digital or electronic form.

The Director General for Taxes made this announcement on April 5, 2023, calling on taxpayers to take advantage of the extra time to make appropriate adjustments.

This is especially important for those who have already submitted their Statistical and Tax Returns (STR), as they may need to make changes to avoid penalties for non-compliance with legislation.

It should be noted that this is the second extension granted to taxpayers. The first extension, which ran until March 27, 2023, was granted to allow businesses more time to prepare their tax returns for submission.

This latest extension provides additional time for businesses to make the transition to digital records and ensure compliance with tax legislation.
