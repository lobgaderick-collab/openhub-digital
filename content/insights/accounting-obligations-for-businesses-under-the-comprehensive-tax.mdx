While the comprehensive tax system in Cameroon simplifies tax compliance for small businesses, **accounting obligations** still apply—especially for businesses with turnover above **10 million FCFA**.

Proper bookkeeping ensures accurate tax reporting and compliance with fiscal regulations.

It is essential for businesses to meet up with their **accounting obligations** to avoid penalties and stay financially stable.

### **Who Needs to Maintain Accounting Records?**

Businesses under the comprehensive tax system must maintain records if:

- Their **turnover exceeds 10 million FCFA**.

- They engage in **commercial, industrial, or professional activities** requiring financial statements.

- They seek **business loans or government contracts**, which often require proof of financial stability.

### **Accounting Requirements for Businesses Under Comprehensive Tax**

Businesses meeting the above conditions must:

1. **Keep Proper Accounting Books** – Maintain financial records in compliance with **OHADA accounting standards**.

3. **Track Turnover Accurately** – Annual revenue must be declared truthfully to determine tax liability.

5. **Ensure Transparent Transactions** – All business income and expenses must be documented.

7. **Retain Tax Receipts & Supporting Documents** – Required for audits or tax verification.

### **Consequences of Not Keeping Proper Records**

Failing to maintain accurate **accounting obligations** can lead to:

- **Fines of up to 1 million FCFA**.

- Increased scrutiny from tax authorities.

- Inability to transition smoothly to the **actual earnings tax system** if turnover exceeds 50 million FCFA.

✅ **Use Professional Bookkeeping Services** – Hiring an accountant or outsourcing to experts ensures compliance.

✅ **Leverage Accounting Software** – Digital bookkeeping reduces errors and simplifies tax filing.
