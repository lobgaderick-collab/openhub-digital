### 2023 Finance Law Changes

Before we go to the benefits of filing early, it's worth noting that the tax code in Cameroon has experienced some alterations in the 2023 Finance Law. A key alteration is the reduction of the corporate tax from 28% to 25% for businesses with a turnover of XAF3 billion francs or less.

It's important to also remember that the new 25% corporate tax rate will be in effect for the 2022 fiscal year. So, when you are preparing your 2023 STR, make sure you use 25% instead of 28%.

Also, don't forget that the deadline for filing tax returns in Cameroon is March 15th of each year, for the previous financial year.

#### 2\. You Have More Time to Prepare

Filing your STRs requires you to gather information such as invoices, expense receipts, payroll, and other tax documents. By starting early, you'll have more time to track down all the necessary documents and compile them properly.

By filing your returns early, you can avoid the stress and anxiety that come with finishing up your taxes at the last minute. Filing early will give you peace of mind and assurance that everything is in order so that you don’t miss any important deadlines.
