Tax filing is a crucial yet challenging part of running a beauty business in Cameroon. From meeting deadlines to ensuring accuracy, the process can be overwhelming without the right tools and strategies. The good news? You can streamline your tax filing process and stay compliant with ease. Here’s how to make it happen.

* * *

### **1\. Understand Your Tax Obligations**

The first step to efficient tax filing is knowing your obligations under Cameroon’s 2024 General Tax Code:

- **Value Added Tax (VAT)**: If your annual turnover exceeds 50 million CFA, you must file VAT returns monthly.

- **Corporate Income Tax (CIT)**: Monthly advance payments are calculated based on your tax system:
    - **Actual Earnings Tax System**: 2.2% (2% plus an additional council tax of 10% of the 2%).
    
    - **Simplified Tax System**: 5.5% (5% plus an additional council tax of 10% of the 5%). File these payments by the 15th of the following month. Submit an annual return by March 15 for large businesses. Medium-sized businesses should submit by April 15.

- **Personal Income Tax (PIT)**: Withhold and remit PIT for your employees monthly.

- **Council Taxes**: Local taxes are usually payable at the start of the fiscal year.

### **3\. Stay Ahead of Deadlines**

Late filings can lead to penalties that hurt your bottom line. Mark these key deadlines:

- **15th of Each Month**: File VAT, PIT, and advance CIT payments.

- **March/April**: Submit your annual CIT return based on your business size.

- **Start of Fiscal Year**: Pay council taxes.
