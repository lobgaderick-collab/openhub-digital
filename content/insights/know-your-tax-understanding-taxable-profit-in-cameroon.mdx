Welcome to our "Doing Business in Cameroon" blog, where we help you understand the tax rules that affect businesses in Cameroon. In this #KnowYourTax edition, we'll explain what taxable profit is and how they work.

**What is Taxable Profit?**

Taxable profits are the earnings that a company makes which are subject to tax. In Cameroon, the rules for figuring out these profits are found in Section 5 of the General Tax Code.

**Profits Earned in Cameroon**

The main point to know is that only the profits made from business activities or transactions within Cameroon are taxed. This means if your business earns money from sales, services, or any other activity inside Cameroon, those profits will be taxed by the Cameroonian government.

For example, if you run a shop in Douala, the profits from that shop are taxable. But if you have another shop in Nigeria, the profits from the Nigerian shop are not taxed by Cameroon.

**International Agreements Matter**

There’s another important factor: international conventions. Cameroon has agreements with some other countries to avoid double taxation. These agreements can change how your profits are taxed.

For instance, if your business operates both in Cameroon and another country with which Cameroon has a tax treaty, the profits may be split in a way that prevents you from paying tax twice on the same income.

**What This Means for Your Business**

Here’s what you need to do:

1. **Track Your Local Profits**: Make sure you know exactly how much profit your business makes in Cameroon. Keep good records to separate local earnings from those made in other countries.

3. **Know the International Rules**: Be aware of any tax treaties between Cameroon and other countries where you do business. These can help you avoid being taxed twice on the same profit.

5. **Get Professional Help**: Tax rules can be complicated. It’s a good idea to talk to a tax advisor who can help you understand the rules and make sure you’re paying the right amount of tax.

Understanding how taxable profits work in Cameroon is crucial for any business. Remember, only the profits made from activities in Cameroon are taxed, and international agreements can affect how much tax you pay.
