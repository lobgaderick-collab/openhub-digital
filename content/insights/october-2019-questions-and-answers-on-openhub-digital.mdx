#### 2\. What do I need to start a sole proprietorship ‘company”?

Let me just rephrase what Varelle actually wants – what do I need to [start a sole-proprietorship in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-register-a-sole-proprietorship-in-cameroon/)? Or what do I need to [start a limited liability company in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-create-a-limited-liability-company-in-cameroon/)?

There is nothing like a sole-proprietorship “company”. A sole-proprietorship is a different legal business structure from a company. You have two types of limited liability companies – private and public limited liability company.

Many in Cameroon confuse or take a sole-proprietor business for a company. This may be because owners of such businesses are allowed to use business names instead of their names. However, you need to add “ETS” in front of the business name.

It should be noted that the business license of a sole-proprietorship is in the name of the owner. Sole-proprietor businesses don’t benefit from limited liability so cannot be called a company. The owners are fully liable for the debts of the business.

You can take a look at [what is needed to start a sole-proprietorship in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/how-to-register-a-sole-proprietorship-in-cameroon/). Meanwhile it is also very necessary to understand various [business structures that are common in Cameroon](https://openhubdigital.com/what-is-a-registered-business/).

#### 5\. I am interested in integrated agriculture and bio-gas

Why not just “agriculture”? With integrated agriculture or farming, Valentine means a farm management system that has as goal to deliver more sustainable agriculture. What does this mean?

##### Integrated agriculture

We call it here “mixed farming”. It involves simultaneously carrying out of crop farming, animal husbandry and fisheries. With integrated farming, each farming component supports the other. For instance, the crop can serve as feed or fodder for livestock, while livestock serve as manure for crops.

One of the aim of integrated farming is to reduce external inputs and increase production output. However, it may not only be to increase immediate outputs, but to maintain a healthy system of production over time.

##### Biogas

Biogas can as well fall within the scope of the integrated agriculture. _What is biogas in the first place?_ It is a type of biofuel naturally produced from organic waste that has been decomposed.

I am sure you get what I mean by it can still fall under integrated farming! This is because biogas is made from organic matter which can be food scraps or animal waste.

The organic matter will be broken down in an anaerobic environment that will help it release gases such as methane and carbon-dioxide. These gases can be used as fuel and are environmentally friendly.
