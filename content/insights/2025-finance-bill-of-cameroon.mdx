The 2025 Finance Bill of Cameroon introduces significant changes to the nation’s fiscal landscape. This legislation aims to enhance government revenue. It seeks to regulate trade. It also promotes economic stability. As a result, it reflects a mixture of opportunities and challenges for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs).

### **Overview of Key Provisions**

The bill emphasizes fiscal discipline, introducing reforms in areas like taxation, customs duties, and incentives for modernization. Among the standout measures are:

- **Tax Amnesty Programs**: The bill aims to foster compliance. It offers temporary relief from penalties and surcharges. This relief is for businesses regularizing their tax situations. For instance:
    
    - **Transfers by Death**: This provision allows heirs or beneficiaries of estates to register properties or assets inherited from deceased relatives. They can do so without paying penalties or surcharges. This is valid provided the registration occurs between January 1, 2025, and December 31, 2025.
    
    - **Residential Leases**: Landlords or tenants can regularize earlier unregistered rental agreements. They can do this without facing penalties during the same period.
    
    - **Voluntary Tax Regularization Program (VTRP)**: This program allows individuals and businesses to declare income or assets. These must be earlier undeclared from the past four years. Participants gain from immunity against criminal prosecution and an 80% reduction in penalties for late payments. This initiative provides a clean slate for taxpayers to integrate into the formal tax system.

- **Increased Compliance Measures**: Stricter regulations demand SMEs to keep detailed accounting records. There is an obligation to integrate digital invoicing systems. This enhances transparency. ### **Opportunities for Growth**

The Finance Bill presents a lifeline for struggling businesses through its tax amnesty initiatives. These provisions can help SMEs clear backlogs of unpaid taxes without fear of punitive actions. ### **Challenges Loom Large**

Despite these benefits, SMEs face considerable hurdles:

- **VAT and Excise Duty Hikes**: Adjustments in these taxes will inflate costs for businesses reliant on imported goods. This will lead to higher consumer prices.

- **Administrative Burdens**: Stricter compliance demands include filing deadlines. Digital reporting requirements also be a burden. These demands can strain SMEs with limited resources or technological capacity.

- **Impact on Informal Sector**: While amnesty programs are helpful, informal businesses form a significant part of Cameroon’s economy will find the transition to formal compliance overwhelming.
