Dividends paid to shareholders refer to the distribution of profits made by a company to its shareholders. When a company generates profits, it has the option to distribute a portion of those earnings to its shareholders as dividends. Shareholders, who are individuals or entities that hold shares in the company, receive these dividends as a return on their investment.

In the context of technology start-ups in Cameroon, these companies benefit from a reduced rate of income tax on dividends paid to shareholders, movable capital revenue, and interest paid to investors. This tax incentive, specifically tailored for the technology sector, aims to create an environment conducive to entrepreneurship and attract investment into technology start-ups, thereby fostering the growth of the digital economy in Cameroon.

Under this tax regime, technology start-ups in Cameroon enjoy a reduced income tax rate of 5% on dividends paid to shareholders. This means that when these start-ups distribute profits to their shareholders in the form of dividends, the tax imposed on these dividends is at a lower rate compared to other types of income. This reduced tax rate makes investing in technology start-ups more attractive for potential shareholders, as they can benefit from a favorable tax treatment on their dividend income.

Alongside the reduced rate on dividends, technology start-ups in Cameroon also benefit from the reduced rate of 5% on income tax applicable to movable capital revenue and interest paid to investors. Movable capital revenue refers to the profit earned from the sale or transfer of movable assets, while interest paid to investors refers to the returns received by investors on their invested capital.

By offering a lower tax rate on dividends, movable capital revenue, and interest, the government of [Cameroon aims to incentivize investment in technology start-ups](http://openhubdigital.com/incentives-for-promoting-the-digital-economy-in-cameroon). This tax incentive encourages capital inflow into the digital economy, supports the growth of technology start-ups, and contributes to the overall development of the technology sector in Cameroon.

It is important for technology start-ups and potential investors to consult with tax professionals or relevant authorities to fully understand the eligibility criteria, reporting requirements, and any limitations associated with these tax incentives. By taking advantage of these incentives, technology start-ups in Cameroon can attract more investment, foster entrepreneurship, and stimulate the growth of the digital economy in the country.
