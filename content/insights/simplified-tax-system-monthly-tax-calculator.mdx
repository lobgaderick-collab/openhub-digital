### Understanding the Simplified Taxation System

Tailored for businesses with yearly turnovers ranging from XAF10 million to below XAF50 million, the Simplified Taxation System provides a straightforward framework for tax compliance. It simplifies tax declaration and payment, reducing administrative burdens for smaller enterprises.

### Monthly Tax Declaration and Payment Process

According to Cameroonian tax law, businesses under the Simplified Taxation System must declare and remit their monthly taxes on or before the 15th of each month for the previous month's turnover. This timely declaration and payment process are crucial for maintaining compliance and avoiding penalties.

### Method of Payment

Payments under the Simplified Taxation System can be made through various channels, including bank transfers, electronic payment platforms, and designated tax offices. Taxpayers have the flexibility to choose the most convenient payment method that aligns with their business operations.
