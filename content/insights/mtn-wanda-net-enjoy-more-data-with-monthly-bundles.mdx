MTN Wanda Net offers you more data volume and a 30-day validity period on all bundles, ensuring you can browse without stress. This personalized plan is designed to meet your monthly data consumption needs.

## MTN Wanda Net

### Plan Details:

- **More Data Volume:** Browse more with increased data.
- **30 Days Validity:** All bundles last for a full 30 days.
- **Data Bonus:** Enjoy bonus data with no stress once you subscribe.
- **Personalized Bundles:** Tailored to fit your monthly consumption.

### Activation:

Dial [\*123\*10#](tel:*123*10#) to subscribe and choose your preferred bundle.

### Balance Check:

Dial [\*123\*10\*99#](tel:*123*10*99#) to check your data balance.

### Deactivation:

Dial [\*123\*10\*0#](tel:*123*10*0#) to unsubscribe from the service.
