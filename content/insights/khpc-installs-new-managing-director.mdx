Mr. Christophe Avognon takes the helm as the newly appointed Managing Director of the Kikot-Mbebe Hydro Power Company (KHPC).

In a significant development for the Kikot-Mbebe Hydro Power Company (KHPC), Mr. Christophe Avognon assumed the role of Managing Director on May 28, 2024. The installation took place following the conclusion of the 6th session of the KHPC Board of Directors meeting.

The appointment marks a pivotal moment for KHPC, as it prepares to embark on the Kikot hydroelectric development project. With the objective of generating 500 MW of electricity to contribute to the national grid, the project holds immense potential for bolstering the country's energy infrastructure.

![Mr. Christophe Avognon takes the helm as the newly appointed Managing Director of the Kikot-Mbebe Hydro Power Company (KHPC).](images/fb_img_17174320511471655737171104925071.jpg)

Minister of Water and Energy, Mr. Gaston Eloundou Essomba, emphasized the importance of adhering to timelines and ensuring the timely completion of the project.

Mr. Avognon's leadership will play a crucial role in steering KHPC towards achieving its goals in the realm of hydroelectric power generation.

Transitioning into his new role, Mr. Avognon expressed his commitment to overseeing the project with diligence and efficiency. He acknowledged the responsibility entrusted to him and pledged to work closely with stakeholders to meet the project's objectives.

The installation of Mr. Avognon signifies a new chapter for KHPC, characterized by focused leadership and strategic direction. As the company progresses with its hydroelectric endeavors, all eyes are on Mr. Avognon and his team to deliver sustainable energy solutions for the nation.

In addition to overseeing the Kikot hydroelectric project, Mr. Avognon aims to prioritize sustainability and environmental stewardship in KHPC's operations. He envisions leveraging innovative technologies and green practices to minimize the project's ecological footprint while maximizing its energy output.

With a strong focus on community engagement and social responsibility, KHPC under Mr. Avognon's leadership is poised to not only meet but exceed expectations in the realm of hydroelectric power generation.
