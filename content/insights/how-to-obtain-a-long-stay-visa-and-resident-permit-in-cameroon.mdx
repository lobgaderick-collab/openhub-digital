Planning an extended stay in Cameroon for work or other purposes? Securing the right visa and resident permit is essential. This guide provides a detailed overview of the process and documentation required to obtain a long-stay visa and then a resident permit in Cameroon.

#### **Step 1: Obtaining a Long-Stay Visa**

Before you can apply for a resident permit, you must first obtain a long-stay visa. This visa is necessary for extended stays and is required to start the process of legal residency in Cameroon. Below are the documents you’ll need to apply for a long-stay visa:

#### **Step 2: Obtaining a Resident Permit**

With your long-stay visa in hand, you can proceed to apply for a resident permit to legally reside in Cameroon. This permit is necessary for longer stays and to engage in activities such as employment. Here are the documents required:

1. **Physical Valid Passport**
    - Present your original passport, which should be valid for the duration of your stay.

3. **Signed and Approved Labor Contract**
    - Provide a labor contract that has been signed and approved by the **Ministry of Employment and Vocational Training (MINEFOP)** and is still valid.

5. **Valid Long-Stay Visa**
    - Your long-stay visa must be for more than six months and still valid.

7. **Police Clearance Certificate**
    - Obtain a **Police Clearance Certificate (Non-Conviction)** issued within the last three months.

9. **Parents’ Names**
    - Submit your parents' names as part of the personal information required.

#### **Additional Tips**

- **Visa First:** Remember, you need to obtain the long-stay visa before you can apply for a resident permit.

- **One-Year Multiple Entry Visa:** It’s advisable to obtain a one-year multiple entry visa, as it qualifies you for a resident permit application.

- **Verify Requirements:** Always check with the relevant Cameroonian embassy or consulate for any updates on requirements.

- **Prompt Submissions:** Ensure all your documents are up-to-date and submit your applications promptly to avoid any delays.

- **Professional Assistance:** Consider professional services if you need help navigating the complexities of visa and permit applications.

#### **Advantages of Securing a Long-Stay Visa and Resident Permit**

- **Legal Residency:** Enables you to legally reside in Cameroon and engage in activities like employment or study.

- **Multiple Entries:** With a one-year multiple entry visa, you can leave and re-enter Cameroon without reapplying for a new visa each time.

- **Compliance:** Ensures you are compliant with Cameroonian immigration laws, avoiding any legal issues during your stay.
