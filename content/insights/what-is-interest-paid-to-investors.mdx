Interest paid to investors refers to the returns or earnings received by individuals or entities who have invested their capital in a particular venture or company. In the context of technology start-ups in Cameroon, interest paid to investors refers to the income or profit generated from the capital invested by individuals or entities in these start-ups.

Technology start-ups in Cameroon benefit from a reduced rate of income tax on interest paid to investors, dividends paid to shareholders, and movable capital revenue. This tax incentive, specifically designed for the technology sector, aims to foster an environment of entrepreneurship, attract investment, and promote the growth of the digital economy in Cameroon.

Under this tax regime, technology start-ups enjoy a reduced income tax rate of 5% on interest paid to investors. This means that when these start-ups provide a return on the capital invested by their investors in the form of interest, the tax imposed on that interest income is at a lower rate compared to other types of income. This reduced tax rate makes investing in technology start-ups more attractive for potential investors, as they can benefit from a favorable tax treatment on their interest earnings.

Alongside the reduced rate on interest, technology start-ups in Cameroon also benefit from the reduced rate of 5% on income tax applicable to dividends paid to shareholders and movable capital revenue. Dividends paid to shareholders refer to the distribution of profits made by a company to its shareholders, while movable capital revenue refers to the profit earned from the sale or transfer of movable assets.

By offering a lower tax rate on interest, dividends, and movable capital revenue, the government of [Cameroon aims to incentivize investment in technology start-ups](http://openhubdigital.com/incentives-for-promoting-the-digital-economy-in-cameroon). This tax incentive encourages capital inflow into the digital economy, supports the growth of technology start-ups, and contributes to the overall development of the technology sector in Cameroon.

It is advisable for technology start-ups and potential investors to consult with tax professionals or relevant authorities to fully understand the eligibility criteria, reporting requirements, and any limitations associated with these tax incentives.

By taking advantage of these incentives, technology start-ups in Cameroon can attract more investment, foster entrepreneurship, and stimulate the growth of the digital economy in the country.
