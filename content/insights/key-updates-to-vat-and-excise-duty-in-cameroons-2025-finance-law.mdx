The 2025 Finance Law brings notable updates to **Value-Added Tax (VAT)** and **Excise Duty**, particularly focusing on exemptions and scope adjustments. Here's a comparison with the 2024 General Tax Code, highlighting the key changes, benefits, and challenges.

* * *

### **1\. VAT Exemptions for Essential Goods**

#### **2025 Finance Law Updates**

- Section 128 introduces an exemption for **essential goods** listed in Annex 1, maintaining its focus on affordability for basic necessities.

- The rest of the provisions remain unchanged.

#### **Comparison with 2024 General Tax Code**

- The 2024 General Tax Code already exempted **essential goods** and basic foodstuffs from VAT, particularly items sold directly by farmers, livestock breeders, and fishermen​Law on Local Taxation​Law on Local Taxation.

- The 2025 law clarifies and expands the implementation of these exemptions, ensuring they are automatically applied without prior issuance of exemption certificates​Law on Local Taxation.

* * *

### **2\. Excise Duty Exemptions**

#### **2025 Finance Law Updates**

Section 131a of the Finance Law exempts:

1. **Inputs of products subject to excise duty**, provided they are purchased by local production enterprises that themselves are subject to excise duty.

3. **Electrically powered vehicles and motorbikes**, under specified tariff subheadings, to encourage environmentally friendly transportation options.

#### **Comparison with 2024 General Tax Code**

- The 2024 General Tax Code only exempted certain inputs used in specific industries, without targeting environmentally friendly vehicles​Law on Local Taxation.

- The 2025 updates explicitly support green energy initiatives by exempting electric vehicles and motorbikes​Law on Local Taxation.

* * *

### **3\. Benefits of the Updates**

#### **For VAT Exemptions:**

- **Affordability:** Expanded VAT exemptions for essential goods lower the cost of basic necessities for households.

- **Streamlined Compliance:** Automatic application of exemptions reduces administrative hurdles for businesses.

#### **For Excise Duty Exemptions:**

- **Support for Local Production:** Exempting inputs used by local enterprises boosts competitiveness and reduces production costs.

- **Environmental Incentives:** Exemptions for electric vehicles promote the adoption of cleaner technologies and align with global sustainability goals.

* * *

### **4\. Challenges of the Updates**

1. **Administrative Complexity:** Applying exemptions, particularly for electric vehicles and specific inputs, requires clear regulations and monitoring mechanisms.

3. **Potential for Misuse:** Without proper oversight, exemptions could be exploited by entities not qualifying for the benefits.

5. **Implementation Delays:** Businesses may face delays in accessing exemptions due to gaps in regulatory frameworks.

* * *

### **5\. Practical Examples**

**Example 1: Electric Vehicle Importer** An importer bringing in electric motorbikes under tariff subheading 8711.60.00.000 benefits from a **complete excise duty exemption**, reducing the final cost for consumers.

**Example 2: Local Beverage Producer** A beverage producer purchases inputs for production. Under the 2025 provisions, these inputs are exempt from excise duty, lowering production costs and enabling competitive pricing.
