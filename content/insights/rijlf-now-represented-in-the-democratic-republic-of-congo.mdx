The Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) has joined the [Réseau International des Jeunes Leaders Francophones – RIJLF](http://openhubdigital.com/news-briefs-reseau-international-des-jeunes-leaders-francophones-rijlf/). The DRC branch was launched on January 4, 2018.

A national bureau to coordinate the activities of RIJLF-DRC was put in place with Alexis Balisha Kamena as the National Representative. Below are the names of those to steer the activities of the Young Francophone Leaders International Network in DRC:

[RIJLF](https://www.rijlf.com/objectifs) (Young Francophone Leaders International Network), is an international organization that is engaged in leadership development in French speaking countries in the world has launched its Cameroon branch.

\[trend-ad-shortcode id="4512" title="Leaderboard Google Adsense"\]

RIJLF is already found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Congo Brazzaville, Côte d’Ivoire, Guinea Conakry, France, Haïti, Mauritania, Niger, Democratic Republic of Congo, Senegal, Tchad and Togo.
